## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR RETAIL TRADERS

### The Reality Check:
- **90-95% of day traders lose money** - this is statistically proven
- Most successful "traders" make more from selling courses than trading
- The boring strategies (index funds, dividends) often outperform active trading

### If You Must Day Trade:
1. **Start with at least $25,000** (PDT rule) or use offshore broker
2. **Paper trade for 3-6 months minimum** before risking real money
3. **Risk no more than 1% per trade**
4. **Have a written trading plan** with specific rules
5. **Track every trade** in a journal

### Recommended Path for Retail Traders:
1. **Years 1-2:** Index funds (VTI/VOO) while learning
2. **Years 2-3:** Add dividend growth stocks
3. **Year 3+:** If still interested, small-scale day trading with <10% of portfolio

### Red Flags to Avoid:
- Promises of getting rich quick
- No verified track record
- Pressure to buy expensive courses
- "Secret" strategies
- No discussion of risk or losses
