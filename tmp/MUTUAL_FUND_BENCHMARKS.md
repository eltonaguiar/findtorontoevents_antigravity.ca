## 9. Academic Research on Crypto Strategy Returns

### Key Papers and Findings

1. **"Do Technical Trading Rules Outperform Buy-and-Hold in Crypto?"** (2024, JDQS)
   - Tested 99 cryptocurrencies with single and combination indicators
   - Result: NO statistically significant outperformance after transaction costs
   - Implication: Simple TA is not enough

2. **"Trading Games: Beating Passive Strategies in Bullish Crypto Markets"** (2025, Journal of Futures Markets)
   - Pairs trading and optimized strategies CAN beat buy-and-hold
   - But ONLY with sophisticated execution and proper risk management
   - Key: Strategies work in specific regimes, not universally

3. **"Cryptocurrency Trading: A Comprehensive Survey"** (2021, Financial Innovation, Springer)
   - Systematic review of all crypto trading approaches
   - ML/DL approaches show promise but suffer from overfitting
   - Transaction costs erode 30-70% of backtest profits

4. **Liu et al. (2022, JFE) - Cross-Sectional Momentum**
   - Crypto momentum factor: long top decile, short bottom decile
   - Sharpe ratio ~2.1 in sample (likely ~1.0-1.5 out-of-sample)
   - One of the strongest documented crypto factors

5. **Backtest-to-Live Degradation (Industry Consensus)**
   - Apply 0.05-0.1% slippage per trade for top-10 coins
   - Apply 0.2-0.5% slippage for altcoins outside top-50
   - Model 2x historical spread for realistic execution
   - Add 100-200ms API latency for live trading

### Honest Assessment

To claim our strategies are "working," we need to demonstrate IN LIVE TRADING (not backtests):

1. **Beat the GIC rate** (3.4%) -- absolute minimum, or why bother?
2. **Beat SPY** (10.5% long-term) -- otherwise just buy the index
3. **Beat BTC buy-and-hold** (14-50% depending on regime) -- otherwise just hold Bitcoin
4. **Sharpe > 0.8** -- risk-adjusted returns that justify the effort
5. **Survive > 6 months** in live trading -- proving it's not just luck
6. **Max drawdown < -30%** -- stomach-able for a retail investor

### Red Flags to Watch For

- Backtest Sharpe > 3.0 (almost certainly overfitted)
- Win rate > 70% with no losing months (too good to be true)
- No accounting for slippage, fees, spread
- Curve-fitted to a specific market regime (2020-2021 bull run)
- Strategy that only works on one asset or timeframe
- No out-of-sample or walk-forward testing
