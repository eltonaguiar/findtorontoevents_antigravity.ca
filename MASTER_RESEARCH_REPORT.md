## ❌ WHAT DOESN'T WORK

### Strategies That Fail (Commission Killers)
1. **Bollinger Band Scalping** - 37-41% win rate, loses on all symbols
2. **Support/Resistance Scalping** - Commission = 8,119% of profits
3. **High-Frequency Scalping** - Retail cannot overcome costs

### What Retail CANNOT Replicate
- Nanosecond latency (institutional HFT)
- FPGA hardware ($70,000-$100,000)
- Co-location ($10,000-$25,000/month)
- Multi-venue arbitrage
- Order book scalping (Level 2 data)

### Red Flags in Social Media
- Only posting wins, never losses
- No broker screenshots
- Selling courses while claiming trading success
- Unrealistic returns (>100% monthly)
- "DM for signals"
- No specific entry/exit rules
