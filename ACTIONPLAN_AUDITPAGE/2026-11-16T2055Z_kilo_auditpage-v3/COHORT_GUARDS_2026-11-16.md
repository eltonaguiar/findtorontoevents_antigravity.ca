## Why these two

PR #852 stopped artifact sleeves at the **display** layer (Money-Ready top list). That is a backstop, not a fix: the impossible cohorts are still generated, still counted in aggregates, and still exported to every consumer of `pf_registry.json`. Likewise the published win-rate confidence intervals assume independent trades, while resolutions cluster heavily by day.

This PR adds the two guards **where the numbers are produced**.

---

## Fix A — `impossible_winrate` flag in `tools/build_pf_registry.py`

`_finalize()` now emits, at every aggregation level (class / strategy / symbol / date):

```
"impossible_winrate": n >= 30 AND losses == 0 AND wins >= 30
```

`IMPOSSIBLE_WINRATE_MIN_N = 30` is a module constant. Rationale: under a true 50% win rate, 30 consecutive wins has probability ≈ 9×10⁻¹⁰ — a 30-0 cohort is a resolution artifact (fixed-TP labelling, or stops never evaluated in path order), not edge.

Observed live in the committed registry:
- `ADX Trend Confirmation / BTCUSDT` — **91W / 0L**
- `GainerPredictor1hStrategy / AVAXUSDT` — **48W / 0L**
- all 34 strategies at n≥100 were `is_single_source_artifact: true`

Also added `counts.impossible_winrate_cohorts` so the registry publishes how many symbol-level cohorts tripped the guard.

**Not a delete.** Rows are flagged, never dropped — the never-kill / quarantine-not-delete policy holds. Consumers can now filter on a machine-readable field instead of eyeballing WR.

## Fix B — `n_eff_lower` + day-clustered CI in `tools/build_intrabar_truth_by_class.py`

Per class, additive keys:

- `n_eff_lower` = number of distinct resolution days (one independent observation per day is the honest upper bound, since same-day picks share the market move)
- `wr_ci95_eff_lower` / `wr_ci95_eff_upper` = 95% Wilson interval computed **on that effective n**

Headline `n`, `wr_pct`, `pf`, `verdict` are unchanged — this is additive only.
