Ledger Benchmark Proof

Every closed-pick ledger, measured against its own same-window benchmark and a random-entry control.
Read-only benchmark view. This page contains no picks, no entry or exit signals, and no money-ready verdicts. The headline measure is the paired benchmark gate: does a ledger beat its own same-window benchmark with ≥95% bootstrap confidence. PF alone overrates. Measurement ≠ permission: a ledger clearing this gate can still be a blocked or resolution-flagged family (e.g. forex_rsi2_mean_reversion passes here on a same-batch cohort yet is blocklisted in three places — see reports/2026-10-20-weekly-picks-snipe-findings.md); this panel never overrides blocklists, kill switches, or money-ready gates.
