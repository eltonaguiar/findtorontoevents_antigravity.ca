What would count as evidence?

  • At least 100 prospective paired outcomes, 30 entry days and 90 calendar days. Rules and candidate selection stay frozen.
  • Net PF ≥ 1.5, positive chronological-half means, entry-day-bootstrap mean CI above zero, and BH-adjusted one-sided win-rate p ≤ .05 against 50% across all seven classes.
  • Paired net excess return over deterministic hash-selected random-direction controls must also have a positive clustered lower confidence bound.
  • These gates are deliberately stricter than positive win rate alone. They do not correct every serial dependency or prove future profits, fund outperformance or money readiness.
  • Fixed round-trip cost scenarios: crypto 0.30 percentage points, others 0.10. These are assumptions, not measured execution costs. Futures exposure is normalized unlevered notional, not a broker contract/margin simulation.
  • Next completed hourly bar open after first observation; 48-hour wall-clock horizon; stop first on ambiguous bars; adverse gap fills at open. Both strategy/control must resolve before slot reuse.
  • Independent Yahoo hourly prices, not stored DB exit prices. Missing data stays visible; market sessions, funding, dividends, borrow and roll costs require further audit.
  • Conservative limitation: hourly gaps, including unverified overnight/weekend closures, block resolution rather than assuming no stop was touched. Session calendars are not yet certified; non-crypto paper coverage may therefore remain incomplete.
