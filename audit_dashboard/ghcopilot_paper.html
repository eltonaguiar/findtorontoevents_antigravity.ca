Institutional evidence is more than a high win rate

Require provenance, an ordered daily marked-to-market portfolio net of realistic costs, matched benchmarks, dependence-aware confidence bounds, familywise selection control and a sealed prospective readout. Sharpe>2 and Calmar>2 are mandate targets, not guarantees. A confirmatory p<.01 needs a defined null and test family; "positive DSR" alone is not a proof threshold. CVaR measures tail loss; billion-dollar capacity additionally requires actual liquidity, impact, participation, funding and borrow evidence. Those inputs are not available here.

This audit does not change the frozen paper candidates, ledger or preliminary evidence rules below. No new historical shortlist has been retroactively inserted into the experiment.

Grok critic baseline: all ten outputs required

Use dated out-of-sample daily marked-to-market equity with actual or explicitly simulated sizing, net of costs. The current monitor reports realized equity only: missing daily-risk metrics are NOT EVALUABLE, not passed.

  1. Trade count, distinct entry/exit days and observation length. At least 100 trades is a floor, not proof of independence or adequate power.
  2. Net expectancy, average win/loss and realistic total-cost stress at 1x and 2x.
  3. Net profit factor with whole-day bootstrap bounds, longer-block sensitivity and explicit no-loss/undefined draws.
  4. Daily annualized Sharpe and Sortino: baseline aims 1.0 and 1.5, with cash return, downside target and annualization convention stated.
  5. Daily maximum drawdown and time underwater, including unrecovered episodes; baseline drawdown ceiling 20%.
  6. Calmar over the same defined window; baseline aim 0.5, with short-history limitations.
  7. Worst complete month and daily worst-5% expected shortfall (CVaR), with tail sample size.
  8. Matched-date benchmark alpha/beta with uncertainty and relevant class-specific factors.
  9. Passive buy-and-hold net return and Sharpe, plus a prespecified risk/exposure-matched comparison.
  10. Time invested, intraday exposure, average/maximum notional, leverage and cash.

These are baseline report requirements, separate from the stronger institutional mandate. A defensive sleeve need not beat passive raw return if its preregistered portfolio-risk objective is independently demonstrated. No reviewed candidate currently supplies all ten verified outputs on a sealed OOS daily book.

What would count as evidence?

  • At least 100 prospective paired outcomes, 30 entry days and 90 calendar days. Rules and candidate selection stay frozen.
  • Net PF ≥ 1.5, positive chronological-half means, entry-day-bootstrap mean CI above zero, and BH-adjusted one-sided win-rate p ≤ .05 against 50% across all seven classes.
  • Paired net excess return over deterministic hash-selected random-direction controls must also have a positive clustered lower confidence bound.
  • These gates are deliberately stricter than positive win rate alone. They do not correct every serial dependency or prove future profits, fund outperformance or money readiness.
  • Fixed round-trip cost scenarios: crypto 0.30 percentage points, others 0.10. These are assumptions, not measured execution costs. Futures exposure is normalized unlevered notional, not a broker contract/margin simulation.
  • Next completed hourly bar open after first observation; 48-hour wall-clock horizon; stop first on ambiguous bars; adverse gap fills at open. Both strategy/control must resolve before slot reuse.
  • Independent Yahoo hourly prices, not stored DB exit prices. Missing data stays visible; market sessions, funding, dividends, borrow and roll costs require further audit.
  • Conservative limitation: hourly gaps, including unverified overnight/weekend closures, block resolution rather than assuming no stop was touched. Session calendars are not yet certified; non-crypto paper coverage may therefore remain incomplete.

Prospective observations (latest first)

ELI5: how long is a paper observation held, and what are TP / SL?

This experiment is short-horizon: at most 48 wall-clock hours (two days) after entry, not weeks, months or years. It exits earlier if the target or stop is reached. The target and stop distances are frozen from the original source and re-anchored to the next eligible hourly entry open. LONG puts the target above entry and stop below; SHORT reverses them. Prices are exit rules, not forecasts. Gaps can fill worse than the stop; missing bars block verification instead of inventing a result. There is no calibrated certainty of future profit.
