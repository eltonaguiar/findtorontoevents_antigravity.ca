Why — technical

The strategy shorts a coin while its RSI sits in the 30–50 band. That band is the "distribution/trap" zone: price has fallen enough to look cheap but isn't oversold, so weak hands pile in while the trend still points down. The RSI filter selects the moment where a bounce is most likely to fail and resume lower. Why — fundamental

Retail markets (crypto is heavily retail) routinely buy "dips" in a downtrend — the 30–50 RSI zone is exactly that emotional dip-buying zone. Shorting into it harvests forced-liquidaton / weak-hand selling and the subsequent leg down. The edge is structural (crowd behavior), not a single news event.

🔬 How we prove it's not a coin-flip each method: ELI5 + who passes

A coin-flip bet wins ~50% of the time. Test 1000 strategies and dozens "win" by pure chance. These checks separate skill from luck. Every card explains the technique in plain English, why we bother, and — where meaningful — which candidates pass or fail it.

① Profit factor (PF)

Bar: PF ≥ 1.5 net of costs
Who passes: RSI30-50 SHORT 2.16 ✓ · high_trust 1.68 gross but <1.0 net ✗ · class-wide CRYPTO 0.90 ✗
ELI5: what does PF 1.5 mean?

Profit factor = total winnings ÷ total losses. PF 1.5 means for every $1 you lose you win $1.50. PF 1.0 = break-even (fair coin). PF <1.0 = losing before costs. It's the first sanity check — if you can't beat 1.0, there's no edge to argue about.

Metric: gross_win / gross_loss on hard-only outcomes.

② Bootstrap CI lower-bound (5%)

Bar: worst-case PF lower bound > 1.0
Who passes: RSI30-50 SHORT 1.688 ✓ · most candidates cross below 1 on day-clustered resampling ✗
ELI5: what if we reshuffled fate?

We take your actual trades and resample them thousands of times (like reshuffling a deck) to build a range of "what could have happened." The 5% lower bound is the worst case that occurs only 5% of the time. If even that worst case is profitable (PF >1), the edge is unlikely to be luck. If it crosses below 1, bad luck alone can erase it.

Cluster-bootstrap, 3000 resamples; bar = worst 5% PF.

③ Ex-top-3 (fat-tail check)

Bar: edge survives removing the top 3 winners
Who passes: RSI30-50 SHORT 1.544 ✓ · HoffmanStrip/Calendar Effect (PF 999) collapse ✗
ELI5: are you a lucky lottery ticket?

Delete your three biggest winners and recompute. If profit survives, it's a repeatable edge. If it collapses to zero, your "edge" was one lucky trade — a lottery ticket, not skill.

Net PF after removing top-3 by PnL.

④ Hard-only n (no soft exits)

Bar: n ≥ 100 real TP_HIT/SL_HIT outcomes
Who passes: RSI30-50 SHORT n=125 ✓ · high_trust n=61, rsi2 n=16 ✗
ELI5: only count real finishes

We only count trades that actually hit Take-Profit or Stop-Loss (real win/loss), ignoring soft exits (timeouts, manual closes). A strategy that "wins" 100% by never letting losers close is fake. Many 999-PF strategies die here.

TP_HIT / SL_HIT from at_signal_outcomes.

⑤ Frozen-exit artifact detector

Flag: wins > 2× distinct exit days = ARTIFACT
Who passes: HoffmanStrip, Calendar Effect, VerifiedStochRsi, mega_mutation all flagged ✗ · RSI30-50 SHORT not flagged ✓
ELI5: the "never-loses" tell

If a strategy shows an impossible win rate (PF 999), we check whether all its wins happened on only a handful of days. If wins outnumber distinct exit-days by more than 2×, the same event is being counted over and over — a data artifact, not real trading.

⑥ BH-FDR & Bonferroni

Bar: multiple-testing correction (q=0.10)
Who passes: top_edges CRYPTO cells survive Bonferroni α=8.7e-5 ✓ (but that's per-cell, not per-class)
ELI5: the 1000-strategy trap

Flip a coin 1000 times and one run shows 60 heads by chance. Test 1000 strategies and some "win" by luck. Bonferroni divides the significance threshold by the number of tests. BH-FDR controls the fraction of false discoveries instead. A strategy must beat these stricter bars to be taken seriously.

top_edges_per_class: 574 cells, Bonferroni α=8.7e-5.

⑦ DSR — deflated Sharpe ratio

Bar: DSR ≥ 0.95 (publishable confidence)
Who passes: every class currently FAILS DSR — recorded honestly
ELI5: discounting for fishing

The Deflated Sharpe Ratio asks: "given we searched 2000+ strategies, how confident are we this Sharpe isn't just the best of many coin flips?" It shrinks your measured Sharpe based on how many things you tried and how much data you have. DSR ≥0.95 = 95% confidence the edge is real despite the fishing. Nothing passes yet — that's honesty, not marketing.

Bailey-Lopez de Prado AFML eq 14.5.

⑧ PBO / SPA

Bar: PBO < 0.05 · SPA p > 0.95
Who passes: used as rejection filters; no candidate currently clears both
ELI5: would the ranking survive reruns?

PBO (probability of backtest overfitting) estimates the chance that your "best strategy" ranking is noise — its optimal parameters would be bad on fresh data. SPA (superior predictive ability) tests whether the best strategy beats a benchmark better than the null (random) model. Low PBO + high SPA = the ranking is real.

⑨ Walk-forward / holdout split

Bar: PF ≥ 1.2 on BOTH 60/40 chronological halves
Who passes: TAA rotation: all time-thirds + ✓ · most single-pick cells fail one half
ELI5: test on data you never peeked at

Split history into "train" (where we design) and "holdout" (where we only watch). If the strategy also works on the unseen half, it's not memorizing the past. top_edges requires holdout PF ≥1.2 AND train PF ≥1.2.

⑩ Regime-both

Bar: wins in BOTH up and down BTC months
Who passes: TAA rotation is regime-native (steps to cash down-trends) ✓ · RSI30-50 SHORT still needs this gate
ELI5: not just a bull-market fluke

Many "edges" only work when crypto is going up. We label each month UP/DOWN by BTC return and require profitability in both. If it only wins in up months, it's just a disguised long-the-market bet, not an edge. For SHORT strategies the mirror worry is "it only works in bear markets."

⑪ Net-of-cost

Bar: PF ≥ 1.5 after 2× slippage/costs
Who passes: RSI30-50 SHORT 2.16 net ✓ · high_trust 1.68 gross → <1.0 net ✗ · luxalgo 1.13 net ✗
ELI5: the house takes its cut

Every trade pays fees + slippage. A strategy that wins by a hair in simulation can lose in reality once costs are subtracted. We subtract per-class slippage (bps) and demand survival at 2× realistic costs.

⑫ Concentration / overlap gate

Bar: no single symbol or day dominates
Who passes: HG=F 751/815 overlap ✗ · FOREX copy-trader 74.9% EURGBP ✗ · RSI30-50 SHORT multi-symbol, no single-day domination ✓
ELI5: don't let one bet carry the team

We check results aren't driven by one symbol (74.9% of FOREX PnL from EURGBP), one week (751/815 overlapping positions), or one lucky trade. If a single bet explains the whole edge, it isn't repeatable. We also require multiple distinct entry days and a minimum "effective n" — you can't prove an edge with 5 trades.
