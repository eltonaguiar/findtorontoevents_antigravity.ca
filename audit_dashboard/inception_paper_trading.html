What would count as evidence?

  • At least 100 prospective paired outcomes, 30 entry days and 90 calendar days from a sealed forward cohort. Rules and candidate selection stay frozen.
  • Net PF ≥ 1.5, positive chronological-half means, entry-day-bootstrap mean CI above zero, and BH-adjusted one-sided win-rate p ≤ .05 against 50% across all asset classes.
  • Paired net excess return over deterministic hash-selected random-direction controls must also have a positive clustered lower confidence bound.
  • One-position-at-a-time / one-slot exposure normalization: an aggregate PF inflated by overlapping simultaneous signals must survive sequential slot reuse and round-trip execution costs (adopted from the OpenCode/GHCOPILOT audits, which show aggregate PF 1.57 → 1.13 after cost).
  • Consistency stress: at least half of calendar months positive and leave-one-month-out mean positive, so a single-spike "winner" cannot carry the claim (adopted from the OpenCode monthly-consistency and GHCOPILOT leave-one-month-out checks).
  • These gates are deliberately stricter than positive win rate alone. They do not correct every serial dependency or prove future profits, fund outperformance or money readiness.
  • Fixed round-trip cost scenarios: crypto 0.30 percentage points, others 0.10. These are assumptions, not measured execution costs. Futures exposure is normalized unlevered notional, not a broker contract/margin simulation.
  • Independent hourly price feeds, not stored DB exit prices. Missing data stays visible; market sessions, funding, dividends, borrow and roll costs require further audit.
