7 · How we prove it's not a coin flip — plain English

Every number in §8 comes from a dated daily equity curve built from real historical picks, net of trading costs, risking 1% of equity per trade (simulated, compounded). Click any ? in §8 for a per-metric ELI5. The three tests that actually expose fake winners:

1 · The day-block bootstrap — "maybe you just got lucky"

A 61% win rate over 125 trades sounds great — but trades on the same day share the same market weather, so they're not 125 independent bets. We resample whole days 5,000×, rebuild the profit factor each time, and look at the LOW end of the range. If that low end stays above 1.0, the edge survives. That low end — not the headline — is the number to trust.

2 · The random-entry null — "would random trades have done as well?"

Take the SAME symbols and the same number of trades, but pick entry days at random instead of using our RSI rule, 1,000 times. If our real profit factor beats the 95th percentile of these random books, the timing rule has skill — it isn't just "shorts did well this year."

Result of test #2 for our winner: real net PF 2.163 vs a random-entry median of 1.264 (95th pct 1.741, 99th pct 1.979) across 1,000 random books drawn from the SAME crypto-short universe → the real cell sits at the 99.8th percentile. The RSI 30–50 timing rule, not just "shorts did well this year," is what produces the edge. Null test PASSED.

3 · The 2× cost stress — "does it survive bad fills?"

Double the trading toll and rerun. If the edge vanishes, it was never an edge — it was the cost assumption.

Honest limitations of §8: equity is marked at trade close (no intra-trade daily mark-to-market is stored, so daily Sharpe/Sortino look smoother than a live book); sizing is simulated at 1%/trade (historical picks carry no size); and the cohort is retrospective — promotion requires a FRESH sealed forward sample (registry rule M-107).

12 · The honest verdict: make a pick, tune the algo, or research the questions?

Not financial advice — this is a self-assessment of where to spend our effort.

Should we "make a pick" today? No. The one cell that clears every bar (crypto SHORT RSI 30–50) is retrospective — mined from history we already have. Jumping in now is exactly the mistake that produced today's three fake "PROVEN" labels. The disciplined move is a sealed forward paper test (rules hashed, append-only, no re-tuning) to ~100 trades. That converts CANDIDATE(strong) → PROVEN-FORWARD or kills it.

Should we fine-tune the existing losing strategies? Mostly no. Tuning 135 losing crypto strategies toward profit is overfitting — you'll fit noise and call it skill (that's the DSR/trial-ledger problem Grok flagged: hundreds of auto-generated cells, nobody counting them). Freeze the generators; stop spawning new trials nobody scores.

Should we meta-research the right questions? YES — this is the highest-leverage spend. The pattern across every win today: the edge was never in a whole asset class, it was in a narrow conditional slice (direction × volatility × RSI band × session × trend). So the winning question is not "which strategy is best?" but "which CONDITIONAL SLICE of a liquid universe beats costs in BOTH up- and down-regimes with n≥100?" That is exactly what the cell-miner does. Concretely:

  • Scale the miner across every class (it found the crypto winner; run the same (direction × vol × RSI × session) sweep on EQUITY/FOREX/FUTURES).
  • Expand the symbol universe — the winners are drowned by a thin, contaminated universe (92 mislabels, 0-price rows, frozen feeds). Fix the data before mining wider.
  • Keep the fundamental lane for stocks — use public mutual-fund top-holdings to source which large-cap names institutions actually own (the "safe" edge), then test conditional entries on those names only.
  • One gate file (gates.yaml) so all agents measure the same bar — the certify-then-downgrade churn stops.

Scalping vs long-term: the crypto SHORT cell is a scalp/intraday edge (high time-in-market, small per-trade edge, needs tight costs). The ETF tactical-rotation / MRBB sleeve is the only long-term edge (monthly, Sharpe 0.88). We don't have to choose — they're different products. But we should stop pretending the monthly TAA is a "pick engine" and stop pretending the scalp cell is a set-and-forget holding.

Bottom line: 70% of effort → meta-research (scale the miner, fix the universe, one gate file). 20% → sealed forward paper on the two real candidates (crypto SHORT RSI30-50, ETF TAA). 0% → real money until a label reaches PROVEN-FORWARD.
