📉 What does "Risk-Off -3% to -6%" mean?

It means the whole market is in fear mode today — stocks sold off 3-6% and crypto sold 16-23%. This is the CONTEXT, not your personal risk. Your actual risk is set by the Stop-Loss (SL) on each pick — which is 4-7% below entry depending on the pick. Example: You put $1,000 in AMZN. If it hits the stop-loss at $234 (-4.9%), you lose $49. That's it. The market being -3% ≠ you automatically lose 3%.

🎯 How TP and SL work (ELI5)

Take Profit (TP): The price where you exit and collect your gain. For AMZN: entry $246 → TP $280 = you make +$34/share (+13.8%).

Stop-Loss (SL): The price where you exit automatically to limit your loss. For AMZN: SL $234 = max loss -$12/share (-4.9%).

R:R Ratio: AMZN's is 2.8:1 — for every $1 you can lose, you can make $2.80. Professional traders want R:R ≥ 1.5.

📐 Methodology — What We Check (How Sure Are We?)

1. Momentum (30% weight): We look at 3-month, 1-month, and 5-day price returns. A stock that's been going up for 3 months but dropped this week = "TREND+DIP" — historically a buy signal (used by AQR Capital, one of the world's largest quant hedge funds).
2. RSI — Relative Strength Index (20%): Measures if a stock got sold too hard recently. Below 35 = oversold = rubber band stretched. Above 70 = overbought = might drop. Same indicator used by every major trading desk.
3. Bollinger Bands (15%): A statistical envelope around the price. When price hits the lower band, it's 2 standard deviations below recent average — historically bounces back ~60-70% of the time.
4. Wall Street Analyst Consensus (25%): We use FMP API to count how many of 50-110 professional analysts say BUY vs HOLD vs SELL and what their average price target is. 84/94 saying STRONG_BUY with $307 target is meaningful signal.
5. Piotroski Score (company health, 0-9): A checklist of 9 financial health signals: Is the company profitable? Growing? Reducing debt? Generating cash? AAPL scores 9/9 (perfect). META scores 4/9 (below average).
6. Altman-Z (bankruptcy risk): Formula combining 5 financial ratios. Above 3.0 = very safe. All our approved picks score above 5.0.
7. AI Swarm Cross-Review: Each equity pick was reviewed by 4+ independent AI models from different families (Meta LLaMA 3.3, Meta LLaMA 4 Scout, Alibaba Qwen3-32b, and paid-mode-large via local proxy). A pick needs 2/3+ to APPROVE to be recommended. META was unanimously rejected (4/4).
