🧠 How Each Pick Is Generated — Full Multi-Factor Methodology

Every pick is scored on ALL 5 factors below simultaneously. No pick gets a pass without all factors evaluated:
🔵 Momentum (30% weight): 3-month / 1-month / 5-day returns. A stock up 15% in 3 months that pulls back -6% in 5 days = TREND+DIP (buy the dip in an uptrend). Data: yfinance daily OHLCV
🟢 Mean Reversion (20% weight): RSI (14-period) + Bollinger Band %-B position. RSI <35 + price at lower band = statistically significant oversold bounce setup. Data: yfinance daily OHLCV
🟡 Analyst Consensus (25% weight): Wall Street recommendationMean + targetMeanPrice. STRONG_BUY with 25%+ upside = high-conviction fundamental overlay. Data: yfinance .info (40+ analysts per stock)
🔴 Vol-Adjusted Safety (15% weight): Realized vol (RVOL) and ATR sizing. Low vol (TLT 9%) = 8% position. High vol (NVDA 46%) = 2% position. Kelly x Risk-Parity sizing
⚪ DB Edge Overlay (10% weight): Our own resolved pick history from at_pick_outcomes (39,880 closed trades). Symbols with n≥5 closed trades and WR>55% get a bonus. Data: ejaguiar1_stocks

📉 What does "Risk-Off -3% to -6%" mean?

It means the whole market is in fear mode today — stocks sold off 3-6% and crypto sold 16-23%. This is the CONTEXT, not your personal risk. Your actual risk is set by the Stop-Loss (SL) on each pick — which is 4-7% below entry depending on the pick. Example: You put $1,000 in AMZN. If it hits the stop-loss at $234 (-4.9%), you lose $49. That's it. The market being -3% ≠ you automatically lose 3%.

🎯 How TP and SL work (ELI5)

Take Profit (TP): The price where you exit and collect your gain. For AMZN: entry $246 → TP $280 = you make +$34/share (+13.8%).

Stop-Loss (SL): The price where you exit automatically to limit your loss. For AMZN: SL $234 = max loss -$12/share (-4.9%).

R:R Ratio: AMZN's is 2.8:1 — for every $1 you can lose, you can make $2.80. Professional traders want R:R ≥ 1.5.
