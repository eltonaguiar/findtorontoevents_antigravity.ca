10-step readiness gate (Lopez de Prado AFML + Codex governance)

  1. Pre-register — entry signal + exit rule + sizing committed in writing before any data look
  2. In-sample backtest — PF≥1.5, WR≥55, MDD≤10 on the candidate
  3. Walk-forward analysis — OOS WR within ±10pp of in-sample, WFE>60%
  4. CPCV (combinatorial purged cross-validation) — mean OOS WR≥75% across 10 folds, worst fold≥60%
  5. DSR (deflated Sharpe ratio) — tools/anti_overfit_audit_sidecar.py → DSR≥0.95 at conservative n_trials
  6. Break-tests — pre/post-2020 regimes, low-vol vs high-vol regimes, single asset removal
  7. Sensitivity sweep — parameter robustness within ±20% of optimal
  8. Transaction-cost overlay — net per-trade economics survive realistic slippage
  9. 3-6 month paper-pilot — paper_pilot.html tracker, WR within ±10pp of backtest
  10. Graduated sizing — 5% → 25% → 100% over 3+ months, halted on any decay alert
