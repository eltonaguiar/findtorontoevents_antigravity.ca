❓ Having trouble with the jargon? Quick glossary (hover or expand to learn)
PF (Profit Factor)How much you make divided by how much you lose. PF=2 means you make $2 for every $1 you lose. PF must be >1.0 to be profitable.
WR (Win Rate)What % of trades are winners. 60% WR means 60 out of 100 trades end in profit.
MaxDD (Max Drawdown)The worst peak-to-trough drop. 49% MaxDD means at one point your money was almost cut in half.
CI-LB (CI Lower Bound)If we replayed history 10,000 times, the lowest PF we'd see 95% of the time. Must be above 1.0 to say edge is "real."
DSR (Deflated Sharpe Ratio)Sharpe ratio after accounting for testing 2,000+ strategies. If 1,999 are fake, one will look good by luck alone — DSR corrects for that.
RSI (Relative Strength Index)A "momentum speedometer" from 0-100. Below 30 = extremely oversold (price crashed hard). 30-50 = modestly oversold. 50-70 = neutral.
SHORT positionBetting a price goes DOWN. You profit when the asset falls. Opposite of "LONG" (betting it goes up).
13F filingQuarterly disclosure that hedge funds file showing what stocks they bought/sold. We track new institutional buying as a signal.

🔧 Technical reasoning — WHY RSI 30-50 SHORT beats everything else
ComponentPlain-language explanationELI5
RSI(14,1h) = 30-50
RSI measures "how oversold is this coin?" On 1-hour bars, RSI below 30 = extremely oversold, 30-50 = modestly oversold, 50-70 = neutral, 70+ = overbought. Going SHORT when RSI is already in the 30-50 (oversold) zone sounds counterintuitive — but it captures the moment when a brief relief bounce stalls and the downtrend resumes. The RSI 50-70 SHORT condition only works during established downtrends; RSI 30-50 SHORT works because the next 1h candle often confirms the bounce failed.
Like selling a stock that just bounced 5% off its lows — it often drops back as sellers return.
Direction = SHORT
In crypto bear markets (May-Jul 2026), altcoins get washed out to RSI 30-40 and then "dead cat bounce" to 45-50 before continuing down. SHORT captures that second leg.
Shorting = betting the price goes down. When a coin bounces off a deep discount, it often keeps going down.
Why NOT RSI 50-70 SHORT? (the refuted sibling)
RSI 50-70 SHORT has net PF 0.951 — it LOSES money. It only works when BTC is in a downtrend (wins in May-Jul, loses in Aug-Oct). RSI 30-50 SHORT wins in BOTH regimes (it's a mean-reversion signal, not a trend-following one).
The 50-70 range SHORT is regime-confined. The 30-50 SHORT is regime-agnostic.
momentum-WITH variant (F2 WITH)
Adding "price momentum confirms the SHORT direction" boosts net PF to 2.475 (n=102). STRONGER signal, fewer samples.
Only short when momentum agrees the trend is down. Filter within a filter.
💰 Fundamental / economic rationale
Why this edge exists (economic story):
  1. Leverage-cascade liquidations: When altcoins dump hard (RSI → 30), leveraged longs get liquidated. The brief relief bounce to RSI ~45-50 triggers secondary liquidations → cascading SHORT opportunity.
  2. Funding rate dynamics: During dumps, perps funding turns deeply negative. Retail shorts pay positive funding but the cascade continues. Entry at RSI 30-50 catches the next wave.
  3. Liquidity evaporation: Low-liquidity altcoins gap down hard. SHORT entered at RSI 30-50 (not at the bottom) avoids the "catching a falling knife" risk.
  4. Contrarian vs trend-following: Most crypto strategies are LONG (trend-following). Going SHORT in oversold conditions is contrarian to the dominant paradigm → less crowded, more reliable.
Fundamental filter: Only enter when on-chain leverage ratio < 5% (no extreme long leverage to cascade) AND spot volume > 20th percentile (liquidity to exit safely). This reduces the tail risk (CVaR).
