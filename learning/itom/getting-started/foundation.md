## Your Learning Path

This track follows the **5-Step Rapid Learning Loop** from the ServiceNow Learning methodology:

1. **Orient** — Understand the module's purpose and where it fits in ITOM
2. **Model** — Learn the key concepts, tables, and out-of-box patterns
3. **Practice** — Complete guided labs in a sub-production instance
4. **Prove** — Validate your skills with quizzes and real-world scenarios
5. **Deploy** — Apply what you learned to production workflows

## The 5-Step Rapid Learning Loop

### Step 1: Orient (10 minutes)
Read the module guide's Purpose section. Understand:
- What the module does
- Why it matters to IT operations
- Where it lives in the ServiceNow navigation

### Step 2: Model (25 minutes)
Study the core concepts:
- Key tables and their relationships
- Out-of-box values and dropdowns
- Standard flows and business rules
- Integration points

### Step 3: Practice (45 minutes)
Complete the lab exercises:
- Set up a sandbox or sub-production instance
- Follow the lab steps exactly as written
- Take notes on what works and what doesn't

### Step 4: Prove (15 minutes)
Answer the quiz questions at the end of each guide. If you score below 80%:
- Review the sections you missed
- Re-run the lab focusing on those areas
- Try the "Try It" variations

### Step 5: Deploy (ongoing)
Apply what you learned:
- Create real configurations in your instance
- Document your changes
- Share your learnings with the team

## Prerequisites

Before starting this track, you should have:

- **ServiceNow instance access** (sub-production recommended)
- **Admin role** or equivalent permissions
- **Basic ServiceNow navigation** skills
- **Understanding of ITIL concepts** (incident, change, configuration)

## Your First Hour with ITOM

### Hour 1: Foundation (30 minutes)
1. Read the [ITOM Overview](./itom.md) — understand what ITOM covers
2. Navigate to All > IT Operations Management in your instance
3. Explore the home dashboard and navigation structure

### Hour 1: Discovery (30 minutes)
1. Read the [Discovery Guide](../docs/modules/discovery/discovery-guide.md)
2. Complete the Discovery Plan Lab
3. Run a discovery scan and verify CIs populate in the CMDB

## Next Steps

After completing this track, you should be able to:

- Explain what ITOM is and why it matters
- Create and run a discovery plan
- Configure basic event correlation rules
- Navigate the ITOM module structure confidently

Continue to the specific module guides for deeper learning.
