## (2) Cross-Cutting Root Causes (the prime suspect, assessed)

These are not seven separate problems. They are **one upstream failure expressed seven ways.**

### A. RESOLVER HORIZON / TIME_EXPIRED — confirmed the prime cause
The "~72% TIME_EXPIRED" hypothesis is **correct and understated**. Measured expiry rates: CRYPTO 55.8%, EQUITY 81.1%, FOREX 84.1%, COMMODITY 84.4%, ETF 80.1%, BOND 81.3%, FUTURES 93.6%. Two distinct mechanisms drive it, and they must be fixed *together*:

1. **No intrabar OHLC replay.** Resolvers mark picks "flat at horizon end" instead of replaying 1h/daily bars to detect whether TP or SL was *touched first* inside the window. Expired buckets sit at ~0% pnl (EQUITY +0.017%, FOREX −0.00007%, COMMODITY 4916/4944 rows exactly 0.0). The path information is being thrown away.
2. **TP/SL mis-sized vs. holding window.** Targets are ~5× realistic intra-horizon moves. FOREX emitted ~8% TP / 4% SL (real moves are <1.5%); COMMODITY caps 12%/8% vs. realized hits clustering at ~1.3–1.9%; ETF/BOND are low-vol instruments that *cannot* travel 3% in a few days. `picks_now_professional.py:637-639` already codes the correct per-class caps — but the production emitters still ship the old oversized targets.

**Verdict:** This single issue invalidates the resolved-outcome dataset for the entire system. No sizing decision, walk-forward, or DSR/SPA test is defensible until it is fixed.

### B. BACKFILL DATA-QUALITY CONTAMINATION
Retroactive `backfill_*` resolver passes wrote TP_HIT/SL_HIT labels never validated against live price: CRYPTO 68.8% of resolved, FOREX 90.7%, COMMODITY 92%, BOND 100%, FUTURES 96%. `forward_test_only=0` on **every** row in CRYPTO (14,721) and EQUITY (2,153) — not a single genuine forward test exists. The too-clean NULL-`resolved_at` partitions (FOREX: 868 pure-win TP + 1324 pure-loss SL) are signature label-backfill, not market resolution.

### C. SINGLE-SNAPSHOT / RESOLVER-VERSION SELECTION BIAS
When apparent edge survives A and B, it is one batch on one day. EQUITY's entire May LIVE cohort (n=208) resolved on **2026-05-31**. CRYPTO's "best month" is 76% one resolver version; the newest resolver inverts the verdict. The 4–6× PF spread *by resolver version on the same data* is the cleanest proof that the measured edge is a measurement choice, not alpha.

### D. CONCENTRATION (secondary, real)
FOREX is ~50% JPY crosses (one yen-carry bet). EQUITY top-9 is 6 defensive staples/healthcare (low-dispersion names that mechanically never hit TP/SL → inflate expiry). FUTURES is 93% one strategy (`futures_connors_rsi2`). COMMODITY grains emit hundreds of near-zero-win picks. These dragging legs both starve the resolved sample and break diversification.

## (4) Top 3 Academic Strategies to Implement First (cross-class)

These three were chosen because each is (a) **NOW-implementable on OHLCV we already store** (no paid feed), (b) **already partially coded in-repo**, and (c) **structurally cures the TIME_EXPIRED disease** by using trailing-stop / signal-flip / daily-rebalance exits instead of far-away fixed TPs.

### 1. Time-Series Momentum (TSMOM), vol-scaled — the universal sleeve
**Citation:** Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen, "Time Series Momentum," *Journal of Financial Economics* 104(2), 2012.
**Why first:** It is the single strategy validated across **every** class in these audits — 52/58 futures significant; crypto Sharpe ~1.5; FX/commodity/bond all positive with crisis-alpha convexity. Repo already has `tsmom_strategy.py` + `cta_bridge.py` dormant. Critically, it exits on **trailing stops / sign flips**, not fixed TPs — directly dissolving the 56–94% expiry problem. Pair with Moreira-Muir (2017) vol-managed sizing. Honest caveat: out-of-sample net Sharpe is more like 0.5–0.8 and much of the result is vol-scaling, so size with vol-targeting and modest expectations.

### 2. Residual (Idiosyncratic) Momentum — for EQUITY
**Citation:** Blitz, Huij & Martens, "Residual Momentum," *Journal of Empirical Finance* 18(3):506–521, 2011.
**Why:** Roughly doubles vanilla-momentum Sharpe (~0.8–1.0+) and is crash-resistant — the institutional draw. Needs only `stock_ohlcv` + an SPY-market residual for a v1 (full FF residualization is a later upgrade). Already coded (`residual_momentum.py`, `equity_qmom_residual.py`) but stranded in the academic sidecar — promote into `production_scanner`. Lowest-translation-risk net-new equity edge in the repo.

### 3. Carry + Roll-Down — for BOND / COMMODITY / FX (the diversifier)
**Citation:** Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen & Vrugt, "Carry," *Journal of Financial Economics* 127(2):197–225, 2018.
**Why:** Model-free, low-turnover, ~0.6–0.8 Sharpe per class and ~0.9 for the multi-asset combination, with low correlation to momentum (the diversification is the durable edge per Asness-Moskowitz-Pedersen 2013). BOND carry harness (`bond_strategy_harness.py`, Sharpe ~0.68) is fully built and unwired; COMMODITY basis proxy (`commodity_basis_carry.py`) is coded. Bond/commodity basis is computable now from FRED yields + the SMA roll-yield proxy; true front/back-month roll is a fidelity upgrade (NEEDS_DATA), not a blocker.

**Sequencing note:** Do not wire any of these into production until fixes #1–#3 (intrabar resolver + TP/SL calibration + backfill quarantine) land. Wiring great strategies into a broken resolver just produces more time-expired noise — exactly what happened to the trend sleeves already in the book (`cta_cross_asset_tsmom` 100% expired in BOND/FOREX, `etf_faber_tactical` 16/16 expired).
