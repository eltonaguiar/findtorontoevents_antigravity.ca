The previous memo had to **back-solve** the cross-trial σ from a published `expected_max_sharpe_daily`
because the trial matrix was missing from this clone. It has now been regenerated by re-running the
per-class harness over its registered cells — **same parameters, same universes, no new cells, no new
tunable knobs** — which is a reproduction, not a trial.

| σ source | value | vs back-solved |
|---|---:|---|

| back-solved from the published max | 0.022798 | 1.000× |

**The back-solved value was right to within 10%.** Ratio 0.899 sits inside the "consistent" band, so
the earlier conclusions stand on a measured number now, not a recovered one.

And the SE/σ ratio is **0.98** — essentially 1. That is the empirical form of the earlier argument:
**trials here behave like independent draws, so "~25 effective trials" remains unsupported.** The
shrunk dispersion that claim requires simply is not in the data.

## 1. The effect size that would clear the gate

Solved for SR such that DSR = 0.95 at N=120, T=2514, skew −0.090, kurtosis 7.97:

| σ basis | SR_daily required | annualised | vs the book's 0.06901 | at the book's vol (236 bp/day) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **empirical 0.020485** | **0.08629** | **1.3698** | **+25%** | **≈20.4 bp/day** |
| back-solved 0.022798 | 0.09233 | 1.4657 | +34% | ≈21.9 bp/day |
| SE 0.020089 | 0.08526 | 1.3534 | +24% | ≈20.2 bp/day |

**Read the top row: the book needs to earn about 20.4 bp/day instead of 16.3** — a **25%** improvement
in mean daily return at the same volatility, or equivalently a Sharpe of **1.37** instead of 1.095.
That is the entire ask. It is not a fantasy number; it is a *quarter* more edge than we already measure,
which is exactly why this programme is worth continuing and also why it is not there yet.

## 2. Is anything in the repo near it?

Across the 39 cells with a computed annualised Sharpe in the regenerated matrix, **exactly one is above
the 1.37 bar — and it does not survive one line of scrutiny**:

| cell | class | SR_ann | n days | CI-LB | DSR |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **H-228 funding_carry_delta_neutral** | CRYPTO | **3.205** | **219** | **−0.005** | **0.9494** |
| H-201 xsmom_top3_6m | EQUITY | 1.168 | 3744 | +7.246 | 0.156 |
| H-200:NVDA tsmom_12_1 | EQUITY | 0.997 | 3744 | +8.727 | 0.044 |
| H-206 tsmom_6m_long_flat | CRYPTO | 0.928 | 4450 | +8.452 | 0.016 |
| H-205 xsmom_top3_6m | ETF | 0.755 | 3744 | +2.007 | 0.005 |

H-228 is the honest punchline of this whole exercise:

- its Sharpe is **3.2 annualised** — double the bar — on **219 days**, which is under a year;
- its bootstrap **CI lower bound is −0.005 bp/day**, i.e. it does not exclude zero;
- its **DSR is 0.9494, five ten-thousandths below the 0.95 gate**, which is precisely the shape of a
  result that would be declared "done" by anyone rounding to three decimals.

Everything that *does* have a positive lower bound (H-201 at +7.246, H-200:NVDA at +8.727 bx/day) has a
Sharpe **below** the bar, and DSRs of 0.156 and 0.044. So: **the bar is not merely unmet, the two
requirements — a binding lower bound and a Sharpe above 1.37 — are currently met by disjoint sets of
cells.** That is the clearest statement yet of what "not ready" means here.

## 3. What this implies for the next hypothesis

Since the gate needs +25% on a 16 bp/day effect, and the universe/cost/class axes are all spent, the
only remaining routes are:

1. **Raise per-trade capture** — hold longer (turnover is already 7.9×/yr, so horizon is not the issue),
   or select on a signal with genuinely wider cross-sectional spread than 6-month momentum.
2. **Lower the hurdle's divisor** — add *no* new trials. Every hypothesis registered anywhere in this
   repo raises SR0* for every existing cell, including H-242's.
3. **Buy sample, not search** — H-242 already showed the CI gate rewards length; DSR rewards it too
   through the √(T−1) term. Same rule, more history, no new knob, is the only free move left.

## Limits

- The regenerated matrix is a **reproduction**; it cannot be compared cell-by-cell to the missing
  2026-11-22 artifact, so "identical to the original" is not claimed — only "consistent σ within 10%".
- σ is computed across cells of *differing* sample lengths and overlapping instruments, so 0.0205 is a
  working figure, not a clean cross-sectional estimate. Both bases are reported for that reason.
- The 1.37 bar is specific to N=120, T=2514 and this cell's non-normality. It is not a programme-wide
  constant, and it moves if N or T moves.
