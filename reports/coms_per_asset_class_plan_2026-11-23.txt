ETFs: ETF is the same instrument as the exposure, so the comparison is wrapper cost only: ETF 0.03-0.25% MER, $0 commission, ~1-2bp spread -> cheapest buyable vehicle we can actually execute.
BONDS: direct bonds = Min $5,000 purchase, no MER, but bid-ask on retail size is wide and unmeasured (UNKNOWN). Bond ETF ~0.05-0.35% MER, 1-2bp spread, $0 commission. Bond index MF ~0.30-0.60%. Active bond MF ~1.0-1.5%. On $100k/10y: direct ~ spread only (UNKNOWN) | bond ETF ~500-3,500 | index MF ~3,000-6,000 | active MF ~10,000-15,000.
MUTUAL FUNDS: $9.95/trade is the only trade cost, so the entire decision is MER: index MF 0.30-0.60% vs active 1.5-2.2%. The active premium must exceed ~1.2%/yr BEFORE tax just to tie an index fund -- and we have no evidence any manager we can select does that.
VERDICT ON THE 4-WAY: ETF wins on every axis we can measure (lowest MER, $0 commission, 1-2bp spread, PIT-clean pricing). Direct holding only wins if you have a proven edge, which we do not (0/55 survive DSR). Index mutual fund is acceptable only where an ETF is unavailable. Active mutual fund is dominated by ~1.2-1.9%/yr of fee drag with no demonstrated offset.
