## Root-cause hypothesis (pre-AI)

1. **NOT classic feature look-ahead.** `tools/audit_pick_funnel/top_edges.py` is a post-hoc aggregator over closed `picks` rows. It does not compute features; it bins by tag (trust/conf/rr/fam/dir/score_dec/source). The PnL it sums is the realized `pnl_pct` from the live pick table.
2. **Asymmetric-PnL × small-sample artifact.** Live DB verify (`picks` table, 90d, FOREX):
   - `multi_asset_copytrader`: 709 closed, 115 WONs (status), but `top_edges` sees ~250 in the cell (subset). Gross win 0.68 vs gross loss 3.01 → raw PF≈0.23 across the source.
   - Top FOREX outlier: `USDJPY=X cta_replicator` pnl_pct=**−0.463** (single huge loss), while most WONs are +0.008–0.012. A cell that happens to exclude the −0.463 row but includes a few +0.012 wins gets an artificially huge PF.
3. **Train/holdout split is chronological 60/40 on cell-row-order, not pick-row-order.** When `train_n=5` and `holdout_n=245`, the train half is starved — typical when a `score_dec=S20` band only became populated late in the 90d window. The "holdout_pf=103" is then just the unfiltered cell PF, not a real out-of-sample.
4. **`best_pf_overall` exposes raw noise without filters.** This panel ships top-5 PF cells regardless of `holdout_pass` / `bonferroni_pass`. That is the dashboard cell the operator should not act on.

So the right deliverables are:
- (a) Audit `ml_crypto_predictor`, `copytrader_integration.py`, `multi_asset_test_portfolios.py`, `feature_populator.py`, `technical_features.py` for real look-ahead (just-in-case).
- (b) Tighten the aggregator: require `train_n >= 20 AND holdout_n >= 20` before a cell is admissible; show `best_pf_overall` cells with an explicit **`small-sample, AWAITING_WALK_FORWARD`** badge.
- (c) Walk-forward gate proposal — temporal split with embargo (no overlap between train tail and holdout head, since picks can hold multi-day positions).
