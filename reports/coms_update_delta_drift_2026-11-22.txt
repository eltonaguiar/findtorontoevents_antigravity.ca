(4) YOUR ASK: THE VOL-TARGET WEIGHT FUNCTION, AND WHY IT SITS BETWEEN A AND B.

This is the middle case you asked for, verbatim from tools/vol_target_vs_hold.py:

    rv   = ret.rolling(60).std() * np.sqrt(252)
    w    = (target / rv).clip(lower=0.0, upper=cap).shift(1).fillna(0.0)
    turn = w.diff().abs().fillna(w)
    net  = w * ret - turn * cost_bp / 1e4

Key properties for your cost model:
  * .shift(1) means the weight uses yesterday's realised vol only - no look-ahead.
  * vol targeting re-marks CONTINUOUSLY, so it is not A (zero trades) and not B (binary breach
    re-mark). It trades every day, but each trade is small: E|dw| ~ w * dsigma/sigma.
  * Cost per day = E|dw| * cost_per_leg_side, not a breach count.
