**Critique:** The thesis is largely correct but overstates the irrelevance of deep learning. With n=48 monthly samples, even tree models risk overfit; the real issue is that *no model* can extract signal from contaminated data. The claim that “tree models are already the right tool” is misleading—they are merely the *least wrong* tool for tiny tabular sets. Understated: the severity of the `money_ready=[]` and `FORCE_CLOSED_TOXIC` rows—these alone invalidate any backtest, regardless of model class. The highest-ROI MLOps addition is **purged-embargoed walk-forward CV wired into Optuna** (without it, hyperopt is just overfit engineering). The single thing to **avoid**: adding PyTorch/TensorFlow—they will amplify noise-to-signal and produce uninterpretable, non-reproducible artifacts.  

**Verdict: GO** (with the caveat that data hygiene must precede any model tuning; otherwise, the entire pipeline is GIGO).
