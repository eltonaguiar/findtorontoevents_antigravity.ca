## Method (leakage-controlled)

- **Universe:** 247 large-cap liquid US equities (S&P-100-style). Microcaps are excluded structurally — every name is a multi-$10B+ company — plus an explicit price>$5 floor at entry.
- **SUE:** (actual EPS - estimated EPS) standardized by the rolling std of the firm's OWN past surprises. An event needs >= 4 strictly-prior surprises before it is scored, so the standardizer never peeks forward. Only |SUE| >= 0.5 events are acted on.
- **Entry:** first trading bar STRICTLY AFTER the announcement date (announcement timestamp must precede entry — no look-ahead).
- **Hold:** 30 trading days (PEAD drift window).
- **Slippage:** 100 bps ROUND-TRIP, subtracted from every forward return before WON/LOST resolution.
- **Direction:** LONG on positive SUE, SHORT on negative SUE.
- **Walk-forward:** purged + embargoed (5-day embargo, 14-day blocks).
- **Verdict gate:** records fed through `edge_stability_harness.evaluate()` — the SAME gate EDGE_VERDICT names. ADMISSIBLE iff |eff| >= 0.3, same sign, >= 3 of the scored 14-day windows.

**A gaudy in-sample win rate is NOT a pass.** Only the harness verdict counts.
