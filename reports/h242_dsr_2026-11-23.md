# H-242 deflated Sharpe — does the 10-year pass survive multiplicity?

**Run:** 2026-11-23 · **Artifact:** `reports/h242_dsr_2026-11-23.json` · **Parent:** `reports/h242_pit_10y_2026-11-23.md`

## Answer

**No. DSR never reaches 0.95 at any trial count in the reconciled range, on either σ basis.**
H-242 passes the confidence-interval gate and **fails the multiplicity gate**, and the failure is not
marginal — it is a factor of roughly 1.5× in the probability.

## DSR by trial count and σ basis

Two σ scales exist in this repo and they are **not the same object**, so both are reported:

- **cross-trial** — σ of the daily Sharpes *across trials* = 0.022798
  (`reports/dsr_multiplicity_2026-11-22.json: recovered_scale_sqrt_var_sr`; the independent
  `reports/effective_trials_2026-11-22.json` gives 0.022799, so the two agree to 4 decimals).
  This is Bailey & López de Prado's canonical `Var(SR)`.
- **SE convention** — `sqrt((1 − skew·SR + (kurt−1)/4·SR²)/T)`, which is what
  `alpha_engine.deflated_sharpe.deflated_sharpe_ratio` is fed via `sharpe_variance()`. This is the
  standard error of *our* estimate, not the dispersion of trials, and it is the more permissive of
  the two here.

| 26 | 0.0459 | 0.8748 | 0.9224 |

**Best case in the whole table is 0.9224**, at N=26 on the *more permissive* basis — still short of
0.95. At the reconciled primary N (120/121) it is 0.687. At the conservative bound (362) it is 0.533.

## Why it fails, stated precisely

At N=121 the expected maximum daily Sharpe from zero-skill trials is **0.0592**, and the book
delivers **0.0690**. The observed Sharpe is only **1.17×** the hurdle that 121 coin-flip strategies
would have produced on their own. That is the whole story: the effect is real in direction and
survives sampling error (CI-LB +7.800 bp/day), but it is small relative to the width of the search
that produced it — 121 registered trials with a daily SR scatter of 0.023.

The CI gate and the DSR gate are not in conflict; they answer different questions. The CI asks *is
the mean above zero* (t ≈ 3.5, yes). DSR asks *is this the best of 121 zero-skill draws* (barely).
A programme that has spent 121 trials must clear a hurdle that scales with its own search width, and
this book clears only 17% of it.

## Correction to my own prior statement

The H-242 report said the DSR was "not computed" and hazarded that it "likely fails". Now that it is
computed: it fails **decisively**, and the single-basis guess would have been misleading — the two
bases differ by up to 0.11 in DSR, so reporting one number without naming the σ would have been an
unprovenanced claim of exactly the kind this programme keeps catching in other people's work.

## What this changes

- **H-242 is a replication, not a candidate.** CI gate passed, multiplicity gate failed. It does not
  move into any promotion tier and no allocation follows.
- The 10-year result is nevertheless the *useful* part: it establishes that the effect is not
  universe-dependent, so future work should stop trying to widen the universe and start trying to
  widen the **margin** — either a materially bigger per-trade effect, or a smaller N.
- **N is now the binding constraint of the entire programme.** Every additional trial raised the bar
  for every existing cell. The next hypothesis registered anywhere in this repo makes this number
  worse, including the two added today (H-243, H-244).
