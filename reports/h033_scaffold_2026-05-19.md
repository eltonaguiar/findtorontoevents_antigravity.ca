### The signal

Industry / sector cross-sectional momentum on the 11 SPDR sector ETFs — XLB
(materials), XLE (energy), XLF (financials), XLI (industrials), XLK
(technology), XLP (consumer staples), XLU (utilities), XLV (health care),
XLY (consumer discretionary), XLRE (real estate), XLC (communication
services). These exhaust the GICS sector partition of the US large-cap market.

- `momentum_i(D) = close_i(D) / close_i(D - 21 trading days) - 1` — trailing
  21-day return per sector.
- Rank sectors cross-sectionally at every monthly rebalance date; LONG the
  top-2, SHORT the bottom-2. Equal-weight long-short basket held to the next
  rebalance (~21 trading days).
- **Optional trend filter (pre-registered):** the book sits FLAT unless SPY is
  above its 252-day (12-month) moving average at the rebalance date. In bear
  regimes the cross-sectional dispersion of sectors narrows and the momentum
  spread inverts; the trend filter is the proposal's regime guard.
- harness score field = `|cross-sectional momentum z-score|` of the picked
  sector vs the 11-sector cross-section on the rebalance date (conviction
  magnitude — how far the sector sits from the sector mean).

**Causal mechanism:** Moskowitz-Grinblatt (1999) documented industry momentum
as a separable risk-premium distinct from individual-stock momentum:
industries that have outperformed over 1-12 months continue to outperform
over the next 1-3 months. The mechanism is slow information diffusion across
the supply chain plus persistent factor exposure shifts (rates, energy prices,
credit) that benefit some sectors over multi-month horizons. A sound prior is
NOT an edge; only the harness verdict counts.

### Data sources — free only

- **yfinance** — daily close series for the 11 SPDR sector ETFs and SPY (no key).

No paid feed, no API key. If yfinance is unavailable the module degrades to a
clearly-labelled synthetic series and reports `UNTESTED-data-gap` — it never
fabricates a pass.

### Construction (legitimate density pattern from H-008/H-019/H-026/H-027)

- **FULL continuous-position long-short book** — top-2 LONG / bottom-2 SHORT
  every rebalance (subject to the optional trend filter); every (picked_sector,
  held_day) with a next bar is one resolved record. NO selection of "big"
  momentum moves, NO post-hoc threshold — density comes from holding daily,
  the H-008 pattern, not threshold relaxation.
- **Strict no-look-ahead** — the 21-day momentum used to rank on rebalance
  date D uses closes strictly through D; positions enter on the bar AFTER D;
  the 252-day SPY MA is end-anchored at D over its trailing window — no future
  bars.
- **Turnover-aware cost** — a single 15bps round-trip is charged on the FIRST
  held day of each rebalance period (in + out across the period), not per-day.
  The pooled mean reflects the per-bar average.

## How to run it

```bash
# full run (11 SPDR sector ETFs + SPY, trend filter ON) — writes the report
python tools/h033_equity_sector_momentum_research.py

# fast smoke (7-sector subset)
python tools/h033_equity_sector_momentum_research.py --quick

# disable the SPY 12-month trend filter (pre-registered config: ON)
python tools/h033_equity_sector_momentum_research.py --no-trend-filter

# machine-readable verdict only
python tools/h033_equity_sector_momentum_research.py --json

# re-fetch yfinance data, ignoring the cache
python tools/h033_equity_sector_momentum_research.py --refresh-cache
```

Output report: `reports/h033_equity_sector_momentum_research_2026-05-19.md`.
Data cache: `tools/cache/h033_equity_sector_momentum_cache.json`.

The verdict is one of `ADMISSIBLE`, `REJECTED`, or `UNTESTED-data-gap`. A gaudy
in-sample WR is NOT a pass — only the harness + post-cost verdict counts.

**Known caveat:** XLC (communication services) was carved out of XLK in 2018;
its history is shorter than the other 10 SPDR sectors. The intersect-calendar
backtest will start once XLC's series is long enough to support the 252-day
trend filter + 21-day momentum lookback (~12 months after XLC's inception).
Pre-2018 the test runs on the 10-sector cross-section by virtue of XLC having
no eligible closes — the rank set narrows automatically.
