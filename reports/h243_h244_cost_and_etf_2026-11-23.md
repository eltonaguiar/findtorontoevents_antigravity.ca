## What the pair means

1. **The effect is not a single-name microcap artifact.** Cross-sectional momentum appears in
   exchange-traded funds too, with the **same sign**, at about **one third** the magnitude
   (5.6 vs 16.3 bp/day). Mechanism is plausible and consistent: 10 names with top-3 is a 30%
   each book, and ETF dispersion is much lower than single-stock dispersion, so there is less
   spread to harvest. That is a *narrower* but *more tradeable* instrument.
2. **Both new cells fail deflation anyway.** H-244's daily Sharpe is far below the N=121 hurdle of
   0.0592 and would score essentially zero DSR; H-243 is H-242 re-costed and inherits H-242's
   0.687. Neither clears 0.95, and registering them raised N for everyone.
3. **The remaining lever is margin, not measurement.** The universe extension (H-242), the cost
   reconcile (H-243) and the class extension (H-244) have now all been spent, and the best they can
   produce is a 5–16 bp/day effect against a 9.4 bp/day hurdle created by 121 trials. The only
   thing that changes that arithmetic is a materially larger per-trade effect or a smaller N.

## Limits

- ETF breadth is 10; the cell is labelled `INSUFFICIENT_N`-adjacent in spirit even though n_days is
  large, because effective breadth is 10 with 3 names held.
- W5 on ETFs is statistically indistinguishable from zero; the W10 pass rests on the full decade.
- Both cells are long-only and remain exposed to the market leg; `excess vs SPY` was not the gate
  here (the gate was the absolute interval), which is a known weakness of this cell family.
