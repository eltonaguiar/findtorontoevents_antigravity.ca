## 1. Original Intent / Strategy

File: `alpha_engine/strategies/skyrocket_detector.py` (613 lines, last touched 2026-05-31 cron run).

**Pattern:** SIDU-style penny-stock pre-spike detector.
- Volume DECLINING into a tight consolidation (3+ days falling vol)
- Explosive breakout on >=2x 20-day avg volume
- RSI crossing 50 (40-60 neutral exit-zone)
- Breakout above 3-day high
- Universe filter: price < $10, micro/small-cap watchlist (SIDU, MULN, BIOR, GFAI, MARA, RIOT, WULF, CLSK, etc.)
- Risk: +40% TP / -15% SL / 5-day max hold

**Reference move cited in docstring:** SIDU $0.63 -> $3.79 (+311%) in 100d, spike day +54% on 2.4x vol after volume dried up.

## 2. Academic Backing — Is It Credible?

**YES, weakly.** The strategy implements a well-documented pattern, not a fabrication:
- **Volume-decline-then-breakout (VCP)**: Minervini's Volatility Contraction Pattern (Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard, 2013) — declining volume during consolidation followed by 2x+ vol breakout is the signature setup.
- **Penny / micro-cap momentum**: documented edge (Jegadeesh-Titman extended to small-cap; PEAD literature for micro-caps with float < 20M).
- **RSI(50)-cross breakout**: standard Wilder confirmation, used by O'Neil/CANSLIM and SEPA frameworks.

It is NOT a novel/unbacked claim. The TP/SL ratio (40/15 = 2.67R) is sane and survives basic edge math at >=27.3% WR.
