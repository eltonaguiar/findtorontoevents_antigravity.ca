**A.** The gate in `tools/loop_preflight.py` (H1 sign-coherence + terminal NULL-pnl >200, substring family match against `reports/hypothesis_registry.json`, DNR list, closed status) fails closed on its own checks but only warns on 7d emission dup-rate >45% (explicitly "not a replay blocker") even while the comment acknowledges it degrades forward-lane n. Substring matching on family/id/name is bidirectional and case-folded, so "rsi5070" collides with unrelated variants and permits drift after the initial registration. `--skip` is per-check and loudly logged but still permitted; the gate sits after emission, so it never stops the 69% same-day re-emissions from reaching `at_signal_outcomes` or the stamp.

**B.** The described matrix (cost ×0.5/1/2/4, 1/5/15 min entry slips, 1%/5% of entry-bar volume) has no wired implementation in the replay harness or preflight; `alpha_engine/cost_model.py` only supplies static per-class bps (CRYPTO 8 + slippage) and no capacity_estimator.py equivalent exists in the active tree. On pure 1h OHLCV the "adverse" cases become coarse approximations that cannot model queue position, spread dynamics, or participation impact, so a candidate can survive three simulated stresses while still being untradeable at realistic fills. Requiring only CI-lower-bound >1.15 to clear the bar under these conditions therefore provides little additional protection beyond the base replay numbers.

**C.** `tools/stamp_entry_conditions.py` correctly pulls the intrabar-resolved cohort (TP/SL only), dedups per (symbol, direction, DATE(opened_at)) with MIN(id), and stamps strictly pre-entry 1h features into `entry_conditions_forward.json`. However the dedup is applied only at measurement time; upstream emitters (production_scanner, AlphaEngine paths) continue to produce the 69% same-day duplicate rows that loop_preflight itself flags. Consequently the per-condition n/WR/PF and rolling-30d stats are computed on a deduped slice of a still-polluted emission process, and there is no back-pressure that kills or throttles emitters whose conditions later show sub-baseline performance in the lane.

**D.** Section 3 of the master loop defines the exact five hypotheses and states that the top weekly score "picks the week's lever," but the scoring, weighting, and aggregation rules are prose only; no machine-readable rubric, no automated computation from `build_intrabar_truth_by_class` + coverage counters + source scorecards, and no committed artifact that would make the choice reproducible or auditable. H1 (measurement error) directly contaminates the inputs to H2/H3/H5, yet the framework has no explicit hierarchy or exclusion logic, so the "top score" selection remains vulnerable to whichever narrative fits the desired replay batch that week.

**E.** The `tools/monkey_test_benchmark.py` stub generates 1,000 random (bar, hold 1-48, balanced dir) strategies on the same universe and cost model and correctly documents the multiple-comparisons problem plus the need to beat the gate on both replay and the held-out forward lane. It is still not called from `loop_preflight.py`, the replay harness entry points, or any promotion gate, so the "must beat 95th percentile net PF" rule remains an un-enforced benchmark. On 1h bars the null distribution is wide; without wiring the threshold as a hard pre-admission filter (and applying a proper correction for the dozens of variants per family), it will not catch the data-artifact false positives (CT=F-style concentration, row duplication) that DSR/PBO already missed.
