📖 How Daily P&L Works

Each day shows the aggregate P/L of all trades that closed (exited) on that date in the EST timezone. A trade may have been entered on a different day than it was closed. Click any day to see the per-strategy breakdown.

TP Take Profit — Price reached the profit target. Trade closed in profit.
SL Stop Loss — Price hit the stop-loss level. Trade closed to limit losses.
TIME Time Stop — Trade held for 12 bars (12 hours) without hitting TP or SL. Auto-closed at market price.
