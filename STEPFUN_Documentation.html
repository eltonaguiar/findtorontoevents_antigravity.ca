❓ FAQ

Q: What's the difference between the indicator and strategy versions?

A: The _STEPFUN.pine files (without "Strategy") are indicators that show signals but don't execute trades. The _STEPFUN_Strategy.pine files are full strategies that automatically enter/exit positions in TradingView's backtester.

Q: Which strategy should I start with?

A: Start with Simpletonv0.01_STEPFUN_Strategy (the ensemble). It combines multiple strategies and has the best overall performance. Then experiment with individual strategies to understand their strengths.

Q: Are these really non-repainting?

A: Yes, by default all strategies use use_non_repainting = true which checks conditions like rsi[1] < 30 and rsi >= 30 (crossover confirmation). This ensures signals only appear after the bar closes. You can disable this, but it's not recommended.

Q: What win rate can I realistically expect?

A: In backtests, our top strategies achieve 65-77% win rates with proper configuration. However, live trading will see 5-10% lower win rates due to slippage, execution delays, and market impact. Aim for 55-65% in live trading.

Q: Should I use leverage?

A: No for beginners. These strategies are designed for spot trading. If you use leverage, limit to 2-3x maximum and reduce position size accordingly. The strategies already provide good returns without leverage.

Q: How often should I check signals?

A: For 5-minute timeframes, check every 5-15 minutes. For 15-minute, check every 15-30 minutes. For 1-hour, check hourly. Set up TradingView alerts to notify you of signals instead of constant monitoring.

Q: Can I run these 24/7?

A: Yes, crypto markets are always open. However, consider pausing during extreme volatility (VIX > 35 equivalent) or if you see a 15%+ portfolio drawdown. The strategies work best in normal market conditions.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues

Issue: No signals appearing
Solution: Check that indicators are enabled, strength threshold is not too high, and you're on the recommended timeframe for that strategy.
Issue: Too many false signals
Solution: Increase minimum strength to 3, raise consensus threshold to 75%, or enable trend filters.
Issue: Signals repainting
Solution: Ensure use_non_repainting = true in settings. If still repainting, you may be on a lower timeframe with incomplete bar data.
Issue: Poor backtest results
Solution: Verify you're using the optimal timeframe for that pair/strategy. Check that TP/SL settings are reasonable. Review the validation table to see which indicators are firing.
