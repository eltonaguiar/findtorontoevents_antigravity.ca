🧠 Algorithm Methodology

1. MOMENTUM_SNIPER

Detects momentum acceleration and RSI extremes to catch moves early.

Entry Rules:

  • Momentum Acceleration: 3-period Rate of Change (ROC3) > 0.3% AND accelerating faster than ROC6 → LONG
  • Momentum Deceleration: ROC3 < -0.3% AND decelerating faster than ROC6 → SHORT
  • RSI Extreme: RSI(14) < 25 → LONG bounce | RSI(14)> 75 → SHORT rejection
  • Volume Confirmation: Current volume vs 15-period average ratio (used in confidence scoring)

Exit Rules:

  • Crypto: TP = +2.0%, SL = -1.0% (momentum) | TP = +1.5%, SL = -0.8% (RSI extreme)
  • Forex: TP = +0.8%, SL = -0.4% (momentum) | TP = +1.5%, SL = -0.8% (RSI extreme)

2. BREAKOUT_HUNTER

Identifies price breaking above or below the 12-bar high/low range.

Entry Rules:

  • Breakout UP: Current price > 12-period high (excluding current bar) AND volume ratio > 1.3x → LONG
  • Breakout DOWN: Current price < 12-period low AND volume ratio> 1.3x → SHORT
  • TP/SL sizing: Based on historical range percentage (range_pct × 0.5 for TP, range_pct × 0.25 for SL)

Why 0 picks so far:

  • Market is ranging on a Sunday evening — no breakouts detected across any symbol
  • This is actually appropriate behavior (not trading when there's no edge)

3. MEAN_REVERSION

Detects extreme Bollinger Band positions combined with RSI to catch snap-backs.

Entry Rules:

  • BB Position Score: Calculated as (price - lower_BB) / (upper_BB - lower_BB). Range: 0.0 (bottom) to 1.0 (top)
  • LONG: BB position < 0.1 (price near/below lower band) OR BB < 0.2 with RSI < 35
  • SHORT: BB position > 0.9 (price near/above upper band) OR BB > 0.8 with RSI > 65

Exit Rules:

  • Crypto: TP = +1.5%, SL = -1.0%
  • Forex: TP = +0.6%, SL = -0.4%

4. TREND_SURFER

Rides trends using EMA alignment and MACD confirmation. Most aggressive picker.

Entry Rules:

  • Bullish: Price > EMA(9) > EMA(21) OR EMA(9) just crossed above EMA(21) → LONG
  • Bearish: Price < EMA(9) < EMA(21) OR EMA(9) just crossed below EMA(21) → SHORT
  • Confirmation: MACD histogram direction adds confidence points (positive for LONG, negative for SHORT)
  • Momentum bonus: 3-bar return > 0.2% adds confidence

Exit Rules:

  • Crypto: TP = +2.0%, SL = -1.0%
  • Forex: TP = +1.0%, SL = -0.5%
