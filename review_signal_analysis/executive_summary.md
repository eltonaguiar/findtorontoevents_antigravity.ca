### 2. The Math Behind Profitable Signals

**Expectancy Formula:**
```
Expectancy = (Win% × Avg Win) - (Loss% × Avg Loss)

Example A: 80% win rate, $20 avg win, $100 avg loss
= (0.80 × $20) - (0.20 × $100) = $16 - $20 = -$4 (LOSING)

Example B: 55% win rate, $150 avg win, $50 avg loss
= (0.55 × $150) - (0.45 × $50) = $82.50 - $22.50 = +$60 (WINNING)
```

**Target Metrics for World-Class Signals:**
- Win Rate: 65-75%
- Risk-Reward: Minimum 1:2.5
- Profit Factor: >1.5
- Sharpe Ratio: >1.4
- Max Drawdown: <15%

### 5. Risk Management: The Real Differentiator

**Position Sizing Rules:**
```
Position Size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ Stop Distance

Example: $100k account, 1% risk, $50k entry, $48.5k stop
= ($100,000 × 0.01) ÷ ($50,000 - $48,500)
= $1,000 ÷ $1,500
= 0.67 BTC position ($33,333)
```

**The Recovery Math:**
- Lose 10% → Need 11% to recover
- Lose 20% → Need 25% to recover
- Lose 50% → Need 100% to recover
- **Conclusion:** Protect capital at all costs

**Portfolio Risk Limits:**
- Single trade: 1-2% max
- Single asset: 10% max
- Total open risk: 5-10%
- Max drawdown: 20% (hard stop)
