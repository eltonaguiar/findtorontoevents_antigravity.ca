1.6 HIGH-SCORE PICK COMPOSITE FORMULA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORE = (WR_Score × 0.25) + (PF_Score × 0.30) + (Sample_Score × 0.15) + 
        (Forward_Score × 0.20) + (Consistency_Score × 0.10)

WHERE:
- WR_Score = min(WinRate / 0.60, 1.0) × 100
- PF_Score = min(ProfitFactor / 1.5, 1.0) × 100
- Sample_Score = min(Trades / 100, 1.0) × 100
- Forward_Score = min(ForwardTrades / 30, 1.0) × min(ForwardPF / 1.2, 1.0) × 100
- Consistency_Score = (1 - (StdDevReturns / AvgReturn)) × 100

GRADE THRESHOLDS:
- Grade A+: Score ≥ 85
- Grade A:  Score 75-84
- Grade B+: Score 65-74
- Grade B:  Score 55-64
- Grade C:  Score < 55 (AUTO-EXCLUDE from consensus)

3.2 DYNAMIC POSITION SIZING BASED ON EDGE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PROBLEM: Fixed multipliers don't account for real-time edge quality
SOLUTION: Kelly Criterion-inspired Dynamic Sizing

EDGE CALCULATION:
Edge = (Win_Rate × Avg_Win) - (Loss_Rate × Avg_Loss)

KELLY FRACTION CALCULATION:
K% = (Win_Rate × (Avg_Win/Avg_Loss) - Loss_Rate) / (Avg_Win/Avg_Loss)

PRACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION (Fractional Kelly):
Position_Size = Base_Size × min(K% × 0.25, 0.50) × Quality_Multiplier

QUALITY MULTIPLIERS:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ FACTOR                    │ MULTIPLIER RANGE │ CALCULATION                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Strategy Grade            │ 0.50 - 1.00      │ A+=1.0, A=0.9, B+=0.8, etc  │
│ Recent Performance        │ 0.50 - 1.25      │ Rolling 20-trade PF / 1.5   │
│ Market Regime Fit         │ 0.60 - 1.20      │ Based on regime alignment   │
│ Volatility Adjustment     │ 0.50 - 1.50      │ 1 / (Current_Vol / Avg_Vol) │
│ Correlation Penalty       │ 0.70 - 1.00      │ 1 - (Avg_Correlation × 0.3) │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

FINAL POSITION SIZE FORMULA:
Final_Size = Base_Size × Kelly_Fraction × Grade_Mult × Performance_Mult × 
             Regime_Mult × Vol_Mult × Corr_Mult

EXAMPLE CALCULATION:
- Base Size: $10,000
- Kelly %: 15% (from 55% WR, 1.5 win/loss ratio)
- Grade A+: 1.0
- Recent PF 1.4: 0.93
- Regime fit 90%: 1.08
- Vol adjustment 1.2: 0.83
- Correlation 0.3: 0.91
- Final: $10,000 × 0.15 × 0.25 × 1.0 × 0.93 × 1.08 × 0.83 × 0.91 = $283
