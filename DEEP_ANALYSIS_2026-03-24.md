## 10. Root Cause Synthesis — Why "Win Strategies" Don't Win

The disconnect between claimed 90%+ WR and 32.1% actual WR has **five compounding causes**:

### Cause 1: Backtest Overfitting (Primary)
Strategies with claimed 90%+ WR (e.g. `rsi_mean_reversion` 95.7% IS → 66.7% OOS, PF drops below 1.0) are fit to historical market microstructure. Out-of-sample: the WR remains high but the **risk/reward inverts** — fewer big wins, the losses dominate in dollars even when wins dominate in count.

### Cause 2: Gate Enforcement Failure
Hard-kill strategies (`winner_pattern_precursor`, `yahoo_analyst_consensus`) with **160+ combined live picks** at 0–17% WR. These picks are poisoning the pool. The gates exist in code but are checked downstream (portfolio level), not upstream (generation level).

### Cause 3: Feature Pipeline Collapse
62% of ML features are dead/stale. ML strategies (`ml_enhanced_*`) base their signals on incomplete feature sets, leading to random-walk predictions disguised as high-confidence signals. The scoring system's confidence metric is itself partially anti-predictive (IC = -0.14).

### Cause 4: Wrong Scoring Method in Production
Method A (current) has 26% separation. Method C (ML-First, forward_wr dominant) has 48% separation. We are sorting our pick list with a method proven to be 22pp less discriminating than available alternatives. **The best picks are not rising to the top.**

### Cause 5: Non-Crypto Bleeding Capital
92 equity picks at 0% WR and 11 futures picks at 0% WR dilute overall performance and represent a structural gate failure. The `equity_macro_gate` and `vix_confidence_adj` functions exist in `non_crypto_quality_gate.py` but are clearly not preventing pick generation.
