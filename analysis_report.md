## Observations
- Market orders tend to have a higher win rate for buys compared to sells in this sample.
- Limit orders show mixed results; many were cancelled, so only filled ones are counted.
- Larger quantity trades do not show a clear correlation with win rate in this limited dataset.
- Leverage column is often empty; when present (e.g., 10:1), win rate does not differ significantly.

**Note:** This analysis uses a simple 15‑minute forward price movement as a proxy for trade success. A more detailed P&L calculation would require trade exit times and position sizing.
