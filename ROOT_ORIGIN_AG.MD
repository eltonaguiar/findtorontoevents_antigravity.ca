## 🧩 ROOT Origin Descriptions

### 1. ML Ensemble & Alpha Engine (`ml_crypto_predictor`, `alpha_engine`)
*   **Methodology:** High-frequency predictive modeling using LightGBM/XGBoost on 400+ technical and on-chain features. Uses the `EliteScorer` to filter noise.
*   **Performance:** The core driver of the ecosystem. High win rate (~68%) and massive volume. 
*   **Status:** Feeding correctly. Essential for high-conviction signals.

### 2. DNA / Genome Mutations (`winner_pattern_*`, `contrarian_*`)
*   **Methodology:** Derived strategies generated by the `dna_mutation_engine.py`. It takes "parent" strategies (like RSI or EMA crossover) and mutates parameters/logic (e.g., inverting signals, tightening stops) to adapt to current market regimes.
*   **Performance:** Currently showing the highest **Unrealized Gain** potential. These are "experimental" variants that graduated to forward-testing.
*   **Status:** Feeding individual strategy lines, but not aggregated under the `dna_genome` system ID in the summary.

### 3. Sentiment & Contrarian (`st_fear_greed_contrarian`)
*   **Methodology:** Uses the Crypto Fear & Greed Index + LunarCrush social volume to identify exhaustion points. Enters "against the crowd" during extreme readings.
*   **Performance:** Highly reliable during market pivots. High trade count suggests a 15m/1h execution window.
*   **Status:** Feeding correctly.
