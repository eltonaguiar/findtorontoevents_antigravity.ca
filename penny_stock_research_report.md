### Core Trading Strategy: The 7-Step Pennystocking Framework

#### The Framework Steps:

**Step #1: The Pre-Pump or Promotion**
- Early accumulation phase before major move
- Requires patience and small account focus
- Often sector-based (e.g., marijuana stocks in 2017-2018, 2021)
- Entry: Predicting catalyst before it happens
- Risk: High false positive rate

**Step #2: The Ramp**
- Run-up speeds up with consolidation
- Sideways action followed by technical breakout
- Increased volume and hype building
- Entry: Breakout above consolidation
- Exit: Before exhaustion

**Step #3: Supernova**
- Most rewarding but riskiest phase
- Massive parabolic spike (100-1000%+ moves)
- Multiple green days in succession
- Entry: Early breakout with volume confirmation
- Exit: Sell into strength - NEVER hold and hope
- Key Rule: Cut losses quickly when wrong

**Step #4: Cliff Dive**
- Sharp reversal after promotion ends
- 50%+ drops in single day common
- Best time to short (but Sykes now warns against shorting)
- Entry: First red day confirmation
- Risk: Short squeezes, hard to borrow shares

**Step #5: Dip Buy (Panic Dip Buy)**
- Sykes' FAVORITE pattern for small accounts
- Buying after sharp drops when momentum still exists
- Entry: After 20-50% drop from highs with volume
- Exit: Quick 10-20% bounces
- Risk: Catching falling knives

**Step #6: Dead Pump Bounce**
- Failed recovery attempts
- Lower highs, fading volume
- Entry: Not recommended for longs
- Short sellers target these

**Step #7: Long Kiss Goodnight**
- Final exhaustion before long-term downtrend
- Bag holders trapped at highs
- Stock returns to pre-pump levels

### Pattern Recognition Methods
- **Volume Analysis:** Unusual volume precedes price moves
- **Catalyst Scanning:** News, promotions, sector sympathy
- **Former Runners:** Stocks that have spiked before can run again
- **Technical Levels:** Support/resistance on daily charts

### Entry/Exit Criteria
- **Entry:** Breakouts with volume, dip buys after panic
- **Exit:** Always sell into strength, never hold through uncertainty
- **Timeframe:** Intraday to multi-day swings

### Risk Management
- **Rule #1:** Cut losses quickly
- Position sizing: Small positions until consistency proven
- Never risk more than 1-2% per trade
- Avoid holding through earnings, FDA decisions

### Position Sizing
- Scale up ONLY on best setups
- Small accounts: Focus on one pattern
- Build "knowledge account" before real money

### What Separates Winners from Losers
- **Winners:** Study 16-17 hours/day, follow rules, cut losses quickly
- **Losers:** Chase alerts, hold and hope, don't study

### Algorithmic Extractability: HIGH

## 10. PUMP-AND-DUMP PATTERN TRADERS

### Understanding Pump-and-Dump Schemes

#### The Anatomy of a Pump:

**Phase 1: Accumulation**
- Insiders/promoters buy cheap shares
- Often sub-penny or low penny prices
- Quiet period, minimal volume

**Phase 2: The Pump**
- False/exaggerated news released
- Social media campaigns
- Email promotions
- Chat room hype
- Volume increases dramatically

**Phase 3: The Dump**
- Promoters sell into strength
- Price collapses
- Retail bag holders trapped
- 50-90% drops common

**Phase 4: The Aftermath**
- Stock returns to pre-pump levels
- Long-term downtrend
- Bag holders average down

### Red Flags for Pump-and-Dump:

1. **Unsolicited Investment Offers**
   - Cold emails about "hot stock tips"
   - Social media DMs with stock picks
   - "Guaranteed" returns promised

2. **Too Good to Be True Claims**
   - "The next Amazon/Tesla"
   - Guaranteed 100%+ returns
   - "Buy now before it takes off"

3. **Pressure Tactics**
   - "Limited time opportunity"
   - "Don't miss out"
   - Urgency without substance

4. **Lack of Fundamentals**
   - No revenue or minimal revenue
   - Vague business model
   - Frequent ticker changes

5. **Affinity Fraud**
   - Targeting specific groups
   - "Your friend recommended this"
   - Religious/ethnic community targeting

### Trading the Pump (Legally):

#### Long Strategy:
- **Entry:** Early in pump cycle (Step 1-2 of Sykes framework)
- **Exit:** ALWAYS sell into strength
- **Never Hold:** Through the dump

#### Short Strategy:
- **Entry:** First red day confirmation
- **Risk:** Short squeezes, borrow fees
- **Exit:** Cover into weakness

### Pattern Recognition for Algorithms:

#### Detectable Signals:
1. **Volume Spike:** 10x+ average volume
2. **Social Media Mentions:** Sudden increase
3. **Price Velocity:** Unusual rate of change
4. **News Sentiment:** Excessively positive
5. **Float Analysis:** Low float + high volume = pump potential

#### Risk Management:
- Never hold overnight during pumps
- Tight stops essential
- Position size small
- Be prepared for 50%+ moves against you

### Algorithmic Extractability: HIGH
- Volume patterns easily detectable
- Price velocity measurable
- Social sentiment can be scraped
- Clear entry/exit rules for trading pumps

### Risk Management Rules for Algorithms:

1. **Maximum Risk Per Trade:** 1-2% of account
2. **Daily Loss Limit:** 3-5% of account
3. **Position Size Formula:** Risk Amount / Stop Distance
4. **Correlation Limits:** Max 20% in single sector
5. **Volatility Adjustment:** Reduce size in high volatility

### Position Sizing Algorithm:

```
Position Size = (Account Balance × Risk %) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss)

Example:
- Account: $100,000
- Risk %: 1%
- Entry: $5.00
- Stop: $4.80
- Position Size = ($100,000 × 0.01) / ($5.00 - $4.80) = $1,000 / $0.20 = 5,000 shares
```
