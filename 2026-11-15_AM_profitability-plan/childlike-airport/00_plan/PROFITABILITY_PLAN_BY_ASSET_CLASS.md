# 00 — Profitability Plan by Asset Class

**Premise:** fix measurement → prove (or falsify) narrowly → forward-validate → gate capital. With current evidence, **no class earns sizing**; default `sizing_allowed=false` equivalent.

## Cross-cutting prerequisites (apply to every class)
1. **Canonical measurement:** single reconciled ledger (IDs, lifecycle, side, timestamps/timezones), `pnl_unit/source`, `quote_status`, `resolution_method/kind`, gap-aware fills, no silent `0%`/null coercion.
2. **QA of 0%/blanks:** disposition every open and recently-closed pick (`VALIDATED_ZERO|VALIDATED_NONZERO|SUSPECT_ZERO_FLAT_BUG|SUSPECT_STALE_OR_UNQUOTED|SUSPECT_BAD_REFERENCE|INSUFFICIENT_DATA`) against independent market marks.
3. **Net, honest evaluation:** gross & net (fees/spread/slippage/funding/roll/borrow), EXPIRED-inclusive where relevant, intrabar/next-bar realism, leak-safe labels (embargo/purge), fixed pre-registered windows.
4. **Statistical/benchmark gates:** effective-N/DSR, CSCV/PBO, multiple-testing control, benchmark-relative (not zero/naive), stability across subperiods/regimes, concentration limits.
5. **Forward proof:** shadow → paper-forward with live marks and kill-switch before any capital.
6. **Promotion floor (minimum, pre-registered):** n sufficient (≥100; ≥500 preferred crypto), PF≥1.5 target (≥1.2 contextual), Sharpe≥1.0, MDD<20% (prefer <15%), **PBO<0.3, DSR>0.95 (effective-N)**, benchmark-relative net edge or justified risk-adjusted overlay.

## Sequencing (90-day, realistic)
- **W1–2:** integrity + 0%/blank QA + reconciliation + CI/freshness gates (trustworthy numbers).
- **W2–4:** build/repair MF/ETF/NAV & cost models; freeze 1–2 candidate specs.
- **W4–10:** paper-forward (A: crypto sleeve if reproducible, B: equity/ETF vs benchmark, C: MF/ETF baseline allocation) with live marks.
- **W10–13:** gate review vs benchmarks/costs/DSR/PBO; promote capped only if passed, else wind down research slots.

**Decision rule:** if no candidate clears pre-registered net, benchmark-relative, stable gates in the forward window, deploy (if at all) only the passive low-cost allocation sleeve under risk limits — not the prediction system.
