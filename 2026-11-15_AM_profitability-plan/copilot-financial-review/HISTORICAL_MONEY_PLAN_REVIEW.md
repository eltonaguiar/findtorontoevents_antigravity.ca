## Reusable framework: keep as research and controls, not validated alpha

1. **Truth before promotion.** Pin the as-of time, source, resolver version, class definition, gross/net cost model, data lineage and explicit selection exclusions for each cohort. Show raw, policy-clean and recent/forward views separately, rather than calling one a universal truth. Reconcile terminal reasons with price provenance and missingness before computing PF/WR; a zero may be genuine, missing, rounded or a default. Publish coverage, uncertainty and unresolved anomalies (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-codex.html:52-74`; `updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:520-532`).

2. **Test actual fill chronology.** Validate instrument identity, units, direction, timestamp, feed quality and historical bar availability; use a shadow recomputation, preserve originals, and quarantine nonreconstructable cases. A bar touching both TP and SL does not prove which was first; require finer data or an explicitly conservative ambiguity policy. Mark-to-market at an actual available time and distinguish missing from flat; do not assume future-session-open or daily close was executable at the prescribed time (`reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-commodity_result_2026-05-31.md:33-37`; `reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-bond_result_2026-05-31.md:117-122`).

3. **Decision states with observable evidence.** The Codex BLOCKED → REHAB → OOS_READY → SHADOW → LIVE_ELIGIBLE concept is useful as a proposed, auditable governance model, with class-specific and shared blockers, dated state transitions, independent forward observations and explicit rollback. The historical all-six requirement is a policy choice, not statistically proven optimal or automatically the user's current preference; two weekly snapshots, 14–30 days, n=100 and threshold values alone are not proof of edge (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-codex.html:76-98`; `updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-copilot.html:85-95`).

4. **Pre-register and benchmark hypotheses.** The documents' RSI2 variations, 200-day trend, carry, inventory, yield curve and futures term structure are named hypotheses for controlled work, not recommendations to wire or deploy. Compare matched underlying/time windows, costs, spreads, slippage, financing, futures rolls and benchmark exposure; use walk-forward/purged validation with multiple-testing corrections, stress and genuinely independent forward shadow. Assess sample independence, concentration and capacity, not just row count or MC top-up probabilities (`reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-FOREX_result_2026-05-31.md:47-51`; `reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-futures_result_2026-05-31.md:73-82`; `updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:546-560`).

5. **Mutation before irreversible retirement, but risk controls remain available.** Investigate by source × strategy × instrument × direction × regime; measure uncertainty, failed/unevaluated axes and selection effects. Preserve observational shadow cadence only within predeclared risk and operational limits; a probation minimum must not prevent immediate safety containment. State whether a gate is for execution, display, scoring or research—those purposes can legitimately differ (`reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-crypto_result_2026-05-31.md:41-58,82-93`; `reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-EQUITY_result_2026-05-31.md:56-69`).

6. **Freshness and diagnostic contracts.** Give each metric snapshot age, last signal, last resolution, cohort, sample count and a distinct unknown/stale/not-computed state. Test dashboard and backend contract parity and measure drift tests with fixed windows, documented power and operationally staged responses; do not hardcode a stale prior or equate high confidence with calibrated probabilities (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-claude-code.html:61-73,98-113`; `updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:425-456`).

## Proposals to reject or require substantial redesign

- **Reject clamp as a provenance fix.** The master and May 13 plan recommend PR #876's FOREX PnL clamp (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:150-155,680-684`; `updates/2026-05-13-money-maker-action-plan.md:174-182`). A bound may be a diagnostic or risk safeguard, but can conceal wrong units, wrong instruments or prices and alter historical performance; preserve raw values and repair/quarantine from verified provenance. The document later *claims* the PR merged, while earlier sections still request merging (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:278-286,680-684`); do not infer current PR status or act on it.
- **Reject status laundering and blanket database rewrites.** Mapping every positive `TP_HIT` to WON or treating every zero TIME_EXIT as bad imposes a conclusion without verifying actual first touch and net PnL; preserve exit reason separately from evaluated outcome. Never accept “zero bad rows” as a target if legitimate flat exits exist (`reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-crypto_result_2026-05-31.md:60-69,101-112`; `reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-ETF_result_2026-05-31.md:57-69,124-129`).
- **Reject treating yields as prices or equal-entry as impossible.** The bond proposal invokes FRED for bond/futures exit repair and forbids exact entry-price exits (`reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-bond_result_2026-05-31.md:54-63,100-108`). A yield series is not an executable TLT/ZN price; unchanged exits can be legitimate. Validate actual price source and mark missing observations separately.
- **Reject synthetic lift and unsupported deadlines.** MC probabilities (52% and 64%), “80–100 usable overnight,” “2,000 clean closures,” projected PF improvements, fixed-day graduation and estimated post-scrub PF are predictions or assertions, not measured outcomes (`reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-EQUITY_result_2026-05-31.md:18-24,47-60`; `reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-FOREX_result_2026-05-31.md:8-16`; `reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-ETF_result_2026-05-31.md:41-53`; `reports/peer_claude-phase10b-money-maker-commodity_result_2026-05-31.md:33-37`). Do not repeat unverified `multi_asset_cot` PF, `claude_gainer_st` PF, or old 94%/100% win rates as edge. The May 13 document explicitly says the first PF verification was not performed (`updates/2026-05-13-money-maker-action-plan.md:52-65`).
- **Reject fixed dead/live labels and hardcoded prior percentages.** Missing last-signal is unknown, not conclusive proof of death; old PF/WR and per-source share caps are not current evidence. Compute freshness and cohorts dynamically; report uncertainty. Compare Claude's “all six DEAD until proven live” (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-claude-code.html:61-73`) with May 13's dated status split (`updates/2026-05-13-money-maker-action-plan.md:52-65`); the master's fixed 12% `quan_engine` share is a proposal, not an empirically validated optimum (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:128-144`).
- **Reject “proven” promotion from small, selected or mislabeled samples.** Kimi's pilot asserts named pockets are proven on n=18/38/49 and a seven-check list that was 0/7 (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-kimi.html:83-98,117-130`); the master HC carve-out treats COT as real despite its unresolved provenance/concentration verification (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:104-110,618-625`). A DSR value of 1.0 or a majority of peers repeating the same source cannot cure a bad ledger or shared selection bias.
- **Do not assume claimed shipped outcomes are independent evidence.** The master reports a forward-validator unfreeze, but also later diagnoses the raw-pick resolver's missing input path (`updates/2026-05-11-money-maker-master-plan.html:493-502,692-706`). A fresher imported timestamp does not demonstrate end-to-end resolution or profitability.
