## Executive conclusion

The attempted economic exposures are **cryptoassets, corporate equity, currencies, commodities, fixed income, and listed real-estate equity**. ETF and mutual-fund labels describe wrappers; futures, perpetuals, and options describe payoff/contract forms. “Penny,” “meme,” “AI,” “sector,” “index,” “copytrader,” “prediction-market consensus,” and “DNA” are subsets, exposures, sources, or techniques—not interchangeable asset classes.

The repository does not have one coherent exhaustive “nine classes” taxonomy. Its canonical category map recognizes crypto/forex/equity/ETF/bond/commodity/futures, maps `penny` to equity and `meme` to crypto, and has no mutual-fund entry (`alpha_engine/asset_class.py:101–120`). The multi-asset scanner separately emits `PENNY_STOCK` and `MEMECOIN` (`multi_asset/scanner.py:2270–2275`). Mutual-fund work is **more than an imaginary idea**, because legacy NAV/pick diagnostics reference actual fund tables; nevertheless **a functioning current fund-selection/emission/resolution path is not established by this pinned source**.
