# Famous systems, actual contest evidence and institutional trust

## Evidence hierarchy

Contest wins can establish a winning **account in a specified competition**. They do not establish an accessible deterministic algorithm, independent OOS alpha, low drawdown, scalability or repeatable follower execution. Published historical research supplies mechanisms and hypotheses; it is not this repository's forward evidence.

## What trustworthy firms actually demonstrate

The researched public sources support **institutional practices**, not a verified causal story that a specific firm went from years of zero alpha to guaranteed profitability. It would be misleading to invent a Renaissance/AQR/Two Sigma turnaround or infer every firm's client return from its reputation.

1. **AQR: explicit, inspectable research.** The cited trend paper states its historical method and horizon. Adopt independently reproducible hypotheses, realistic implementation and the ability to report adverse periods. Published simulation is not fund NAV, capacity evidence or this repo's live result.
2. **BlackRock Aladdin: integrated data, risk, operations and accounting.** The [official platform description](https://www.blackrock.com/aladdin) emphasizes a common portfolio view, consistent risk/return analytics and integrated operations/accounting. Adopt one reconciled instrument/position/cash/NAV truth layer. Vendor marketing describes functionality, not proof of predictive alpha or every client's safety.
3. **CFA/GIPS: fair performance representation and disclosure.** [Official standards](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/gips-standards) explain voluntary ethical performance standards. Adopt honest composites/cohorts, complete disclosures and independent review. This package does **not** claim GIPS compliance, verification or regulatory authorization.
4. **FINRA: wrapper/operational and trading-risk realities.** [Fund mechanics](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/mutual-funds), [ETF versus fund comparison](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/etf-vs-mutual-fund) and [day-trading disclosure](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2270) corroborate NAV timing, fees, loss and execution risks. These are not success-rate estimates.

Renaissance/Two Sigma/D.E.Shaw/Citadel are not used as source-free numerical return, secrecy-causes-profit or comeback evidence. Publicly available primary material sufficient to prove their requested struggling-to-profit causal trajectories was not established here.

Some Federal Reserve/OCC historical model-risk URLs returned404; SEC/SPIVA requests were inaccessible. Those failed fetches cannot support claims about rule compliance or active-fund failure percentages.

## A trustworthy transition for this project

| Stage | Deliverable | Independent acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Stop misleading measurement | Complete immutable raw/interpreted ledgers, visible unknowns, priced open exposure | Arithmetic/time/provenance reconciliation; no silent repairs |
| Establish a fair reference | Passive/cash/SPY/risk-matched curves on common dates | Independent adjusted-data and portfolio implementation check |
| Reduce uncontrolled search | One frozen family per lane and a cumulative trial ledger | Registry/holdout audit across machines; failed trials retained |
| Run genuine forward paper accounts | Timestamped decisions, executable modeled fills and continuous NAV | No account reset or after-the-fact replacement; cost/latency stress |
| Build independent risk/ops | Limits, cash/position checks, model alerts, kill switches and incident runbooks | Deliberate failure tests; controls cannot promote their own model |
| Consider regulated real-money service only separately | Legal/custody/security, disclosures, external verification and explicit mandate | Qualified independent review and human approval; outside current scope |

A risk-software company can earn revenue from reliable analytics without its own directional trader beating SPY. Trust, business profitability and portfolio alpha are separate claims. Risk management controls errors and exposure; **it cannot convert a negative-edge strategy into a guaranteed winner**.
