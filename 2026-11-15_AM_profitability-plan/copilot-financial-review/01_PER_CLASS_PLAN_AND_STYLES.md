# Per-class profitability research plans and holding styles

## Scope and ranking

The preferred first two lanes are **fund/ETF allocation** and **diversified point-in-time equity selection**. This is a research priority, not a calibrated profitability ranking or permission to invest. For a short capital-preservation horizon, cash/short-duration exposure is a different objective with lower growth potential and inflation/currency risk.

The nine dashboard labels overlap. ETFs/mutual funds are wrappers, futures/options/perpetuals are contracts, and meme/penny are subsets. Keep underlying exposure, wrapper, venue, currency, leverage, horizon and algorithm separate. Details and production citations are in [the coverage map](./ASSET_CLASS_COVERAGE.md).

Holding-period definitions below are research conventions: long-term >=1 year; position/short-term weeks to months; swing 2-20 sessions; day within one session; scalp seconds to minutes. Pre-register the exact horizon per experiment; never compare unlike horizons as one cohort.

## Plan per attempted class

| Class/exposure | First hypothesis or baseline to evaluate | Appropriate benchmarks | Evidence/data required | Decision and stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mutual funds** | Broad low-cost index baseline; separately, one monthly allocation/selection challenger | Same-currency share-class/category total return, ETF equivalent, SPY total return where US equity, cash | Share-class master, distributions, eligible NAV/order cutoff, fees/loads/redemption restrictions, point-in-time universe and immutable rebalance ledger | Primary lane with ETFs. Do not infer alpha from rising NAV or transplant ETF same-close fills. Reject unsupported NAV timing and selection that cannot beat its passive equivalent |
| **ETF allocation** | Reproduce one frozen cross-asset momentum/absolute-momentum rule; test the rotation before its blend | SPY and each exposure's buy-and-hold; risk-matched multi-asset baseline; cash | Common adjusted history, executable lag, transaction/FX cost, per-instrument freshness, explicit cash, component overlap/underlying holdings | Primary lane. Existing blend has negative-momentum, 90%-weight and 60%-overlap counterexamples; repair/reproduce before judging return |
| **US equity** | Diversified point-in-time value/quality or momentum; one pre-registered family, not today's winning stocks retroactively | SPY total return, same-universe equal weight, sector/factor and volatility-matched controls | Historical constituents/delistings, filing-release availability, dividends/splits, realistic fills and costs | Second lane. If data only contain current mega-cap survivors, no historical stock-selection alpha certification. NVDA familiarity is not a selection rule |
| **Penny/microcap/meme stocks** | Liquidity-constrained event/reversal hypothesis, or report no eligible universe | Same size/liquidity universe and cash; SPY only as additional opportunity cost | Historical price/float/market cap, halts, delistings, borrow, spread and capacity | Deferred paper lane. Reject nominal "penny" labels, stale signals and profits at unfillable prices; no synthetic TP-based short-squeeze evidence |
| **Bonds/fixed income** | Treasury duration/credit benchmark; one lagged curve/volatility-aware allocation test | Duration/credit/FX-matched bond total return, cash, multi-asset allocation alternative | Traded instrument prices, distributions/coupons, duration/credit, lagged yield inputs, futures multipliers if used | Low-frequency secondary candidate. FRED yields are features, not bond/ZN execution prices. AGG/BND are not cash and can draw down |
| **Commodities** | Diversified trend/term-structure hypothesis with actual contracts, not concentrated COT winners | Instrument/contract-aware buy-and-hold or passive roll; broad commodity/managed-futures alternative; cash | Expiry, roll, tick/multiplier, collateral, position limits, volume, COT publication lag | Secondary research. Reject CT=F/NG=F single-market episodes and spot/continuous-series substitution for executable futures |
| **Futures: indices/rates/FX/commodities** | One broad trend or breakout family, volatility-normalized and collateral-aware | Same underlying exposure, funded passive/roll baseline and risk-matched CTA control | Individual contracts, margin/liquidation, limit moves, rolls, commissions and overnight gaps | Deferred until contract-accounting QA passes. Classification is by underlying too; do not double-count commodity futures as independent samples |
| **Forex** | Small lagged carry/trend test only after quote/currency/fee reconciliation | Same base-currency passive/carry exposure, cash and matched random timing | Pair direction/inversion, bid/ask, funding/rollover, session/holiday calendars, credible historical quotes | Measurement-only until repeated errors and recent poor cohort are understood. No universal pip/PnL clamps or forced directional flips |
| **Crypto majors/altcoins/perpetuals** | Investigate the recent cleanable cohort, then one frozen entry-condition or fully modeled basis/carry hypothesis | BTC/asset buy-and-hold, same direction/holding duration random entries, collateral cash and risk-matched exposure | Actual venue/spot-perp identity, event-time prices, funding, fees, latency, liquidations, full open NAV and dedup | Conditional paper lane. Corrected fill geometry is not newly proven alpha. Recent 48h PF=1.678 is raw, concentrated and duplicate-flagged |
| **Memecoins/DeFi-token subsets** | Report feasible liquidity/universe first; no automatic extension of major-coin evidence | Same listing-age/liquidity universe, cash, broad crypto opportunity cost | Listings/delistings, wash volume, gas/MEV, token/rebrand identity and executable depth | Lowest initial priority. Protocol-token trading does not demonstrate LP/staking/lending profitability |
| **Options/volatility/leveraged ETFs** | Distinguish option-informed underlying signals from one actual contract/portfolio hypothesis | Delta/vega/exposure-matched underlying or option baseline; funded cash | Premiums, Greeks, bid/ask, expiry, exercise/assignment, borrow, leverage reset and hedged portfolio PnL | Deferred. An IV/skew signal emitting BTCUSDT is not a profitable options strategy; 0DTE/decay titles are not execution proof |
| **Prediction/event contracts** | Independently measure venue-calibrated forecasts and follower fills; keep contract trading separate from crypto signals | Venue executable odds, no-trade cash, naive/public probability forecast; BTC/SPY only for underlying signal experiments | Contract/event/token ID, resolution/void rules, bid/ask/depth, probability timestamp, settlement, fees, jurisdiction/account eligibility | Diagnostic lane first. No live venue authentication/trading. Shadow only if complete ledger and legal/API boundaries are established |
| **REIT/property, gold, country/sector/factor exposures** | Included as look-through components of the fund/ETF lane, not new predictors by name | Matching total-return index/proxy, cash, risk-matched allocation | Currency, rates/sector concentration, underlying holdings and distributions | Do not confuse listed REIT equity with direct property, or tokenized gold with a COMEX contract |
| **Sports wagering** | Separate Goal #2: event-time calibrated value/closing-line-value research | Same sport/market odds after vig, naive forecast, no-bet cash | Event IDs, odds available when decision made, stake, vig, void/push/settlement, sportsbook rules | Separate ledger and approval. Toronto events listings are not a financial asset class |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## What "wins repeatedly" must mean

Persistent positive **expected** net return over independent forward periods, acceptable uncertainty/tails and benchmark evidence, not winning every trade or every month. Report losing periods and open exposure. A high win rate can hide rare ruinous losses; trend strategies may have low hit rates with positive expectancy.

No future horizon guarantees that a fund, index or named algorithm will beat cash, inflation or S&P 500. Position-sizing rules cannot manufacture an edge that is absent.
