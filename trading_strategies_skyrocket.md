## Strategy 1: Volume Spike Detector

### Overview
Capitalizes on sudden volume surges indicating institutional or whale accumulation before major price moves.

### Entry Conditions (ALL must be met)
- Volume spike ≥300% vs 20-period average
- Price up ≥5% in last 15 minutes
- Market cap >$10M (avoid illiquid rugs)
- No major resistance within 15% upside

### Exit Rules
| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Price +15% | Take 50% profit, move SL to breakeven |
| Price +25% | Take remaining 50% profit |
| Price -3% | Full stop loss |
| Time >2 hours | Market close regardless of P&L |
| Volume drops 50% | Early exit signal |

### Asset Class
- Crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, high-cap alts)
- Meme coins with $50M+ market cap
- Avoid: Low liquidity (<$5M daily volume)

### Time of Day
- **Best**: 09:00-11:00 EST (US market open overlap)
- **Good**: 14:00-16:00 EST (European close)
- **Avoid**: 00:00-06:00 EST (low liquidity)

### Risk Management
- Position size: 2-3% of portfolio max
- Max 2 concurrent positions
- Daily loss limit: 5% of portfolio
- No overnight holds

### Expected Hold Time
15 minutes - 2 hours

### Backtest Feasibility
- **Data needed**: 1-minute OHLCV, volume
- **Success rate**: 45-55% historically
- **R:R Ratio**: 1:5 (3% risk vs 15% target)

- [ ] Paper trade each strategy for 1 week
- [ ] Set up position sizing calculator

- [ ] Check overnight news and events
- [ ] Review open positions and adjust stops

- [ ] Monitor high-impact news calendar

### Risk Management Rules
1. **Never risk more than 5% portfolio in single trade**
2. **Daily loss limit: 10% of portfolio**
3. **Max 3 concurrent positions**
4. **Always use stop losses (no exceptions)**
5. **Size down after 2 consecutive losses**
6. **No revenge trading**

### Tools Required
- TradingView or similar charting
- On-chain analytics (Arkham, Nansen)
- Social sentiment (LunarCrush)
- News aggregation (CryptoPanic)
- Whale alerts
- Multiple exchange accounts

### Performance Tracking
Track for each trade:
- Strategy used
- Entry/exit prices and times
- Position size
- P&L (absolute and %)
- Hold time
- Slippage
- Notes on execution

Review weekly to identify:
- Best performing strategies
- Best times of day
- Asset class performance
- Execution improvements needed
