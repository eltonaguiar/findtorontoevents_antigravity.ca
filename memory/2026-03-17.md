# 2026-03-17

- Researched primary-source evidence on crypto regime predictors for strategy gating/sizing.
- Strongest recurring filters: realized volatility regime, session/time-of-day liquidity, macro risk stress (VIX/rates/FOMC), and structural breaks.
- Evidence is mixed on direct DXY predictive power; treat USD strength as a secondary context variable, not a primary alpha signal.
- BTC often reacts weakly to intraday macro news surprises, but medium-horizon sensitivity to monetary policy, uncertainty, and risk-off shocks is real.
- Calendar effects exist (Monday / weekend / hour-of-day), but they are more useful as execution/liquidity filters than as standalone signals.
- For portfolio sizing, the most robust stack in volatile crypto is conservative volatility targeting + HRP/inverse-vol + a small fractional-Kelly overlay only when alpha is genuinely robust.
- Failure modes to keep front and center: under-estimated vol during jumps, correlation regime breaks, Kelly overconfidence under heavy tails, and turnover/cost drag from over-rebalancing.
