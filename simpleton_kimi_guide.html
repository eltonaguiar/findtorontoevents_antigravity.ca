🚀 Quick Start

Step 1: Add to TradingView

  1. Open TradingView and select a chart (recommended: BTCUSD 4H)
  2. Click Pine Editor at the bottom
  3. Copy the contents of Simpletonv0.01_KIMI.pine
  4. Paste into the editor and click Save
  5. Click Add to Chart

Step 2: Configure Settings

  1. Right-click the indicator on chart → Settings
  2. Select your preferred Strategy Mode
  3. Adjust TP/SL percentages (default: 3% / 2%)
  4. Enable/Disable Repainting (keep OFF for accuracy)

Step 3: Run Backtest

  1. Click Strategy Tester tab
  2. View performance metrics: Net Profit, Sharpe, Win Rate
  3. Click List of Trades to see individual results
💡 Pro Tip: Start with "Multi-Indicator" mode on BTCUSD 4H. This combines 4 strategies and requires at least 2 confirmations for signals.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is repainting and why is it off by default?

Repainting happens when an indicator changes its signal after the candle closes. This creates unrealistic backtests. We disable it by default to ensure signals only appear on confirmed candles.

Can I use this for stocks or forex?

Yes! While optimized for crypto, all strategies work on any TradingView symbol. For stocks, we recommend RSI-2 on daily timeframe. For forex, try SuperTrend on 1H.

What's the difference between the Strategy and Indicator?

  • Strategy (Simpletonv0.01_KIMI): Has built-in backtesting, position sizing, TP/SL
  • Indicator (SimpletonSignals): Just shows signals on chart, no automated trading

How often should I change settings?

We recommend:

  • Daily timeframe: Review monthly
  • 4H timeframe: Review weekly
  • 1H timeframe: Review every few days

Why are my results different from the table?

Markets change. The win rates shown are historical averages. Always do your own backtesting on recent data before trading with real money.

🎯 Remember: No strategy wins 100% of the time. Risk management (position sizing, TP/SL) is more important than finding the "perfect" strategy.
