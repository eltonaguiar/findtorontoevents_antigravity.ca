# 24-Hour Change Review (2026-04-21 to 2026-04-22)

## Overview

A comprehensive review of the changes committed over the last 24 hours reveals a heavy focus on **Quantitative Library Integration**, **Audit & Validation Mechanisms**, and **Performance/Strategy Refinements**. The primary objective has been to harden the consensus engine, introduce rigorous statistical validation (Phase 4), and curb drawdowns driven by miscalibrated signals and zombie strategies.

## Key Findings & Changes

### 1. Advanced Quant & Analytics Infrastructure
- **Open-Source Quant Libraries:** Successfully integrated 5 external libraries (`pypbo`, `skfolio`, `arch.bootstrap`, `fredapi`, `pandas-datareader`) to provide robust tools for Phase 4 risk-adjusted metrics and to fill data gaps (BOND and non-US EQUITIES).
- **Analytics Tools Shipped:** Added `deflated_sharpe_per_feed.py`, `walk_forward_validation.py`, and `block_bootstrap_ci.py` to enable robust backtest overfitting evaluation, Deflated Sharpe Ratio calculation, and Bonferroni multiple-testing correction.
- **Workflow Critiques & Research:** Conducted external quant framework research, critiquing the "Mercury workflow" and correcting misidentified tools (e.g., proper usage of `pypbo` vs. Bayesian optimization).

### 2. Audit Dashboard & Data Integrity Enhancements
- **Archive Deduplication:** Purged 38,399 duplicate entries (87.2% reduction) from `closed_picks.archive.jsonl` without losing unique pick data. Added a dedicated dedup cleanup tool and an archive dedup guard to prevent future bloat.
- **Gatekeepers & Fallbacks:** Introduced a `fail-closed` fallback and `score <= 0` rejection in active-pick gates, significantly reducing noisy signals.
- **Bug Fixes:** Fixed syntax errors/missing quotes in the `strategy_blocklist.py` (specifically for the `non_crypto_consensus` retirement block) and corrected an issue where ETFs were misclassified as equities.
- **Score Calibration:** Deployed a score-PnL calibration analyzer along with a findings report to tune signal efficacy.

### 3. Performance Investigations & Diagnostics
- **Deep Strategy Audits:** Conducted a deep dive investigation into 3,500 historical trades across asset classes, bolstered by a 48-hour performance investigation utilizing an Ollama fact-check and 3-agent reconciliation.
- **8-Hour Cycle Reviews:** Tracked performance across multiple 8-hour cycles (Cycles 7-10). The reviews documented alarming trends: an active book drawdown (-40%), a doubled active book, and the persistence of "zombie" strategies.
- **Top HC Picks Snapshot:** A live snapshot audit of High-Conviction (HC) picks revealed that only 2 out of 67 active picks (3%) passed the HC gate (both CRYPTO grade-b), leaving 5 out of 6 asset classes completely devoid of HC signals. This highlights the impact of score compression and trust-tier promotion bottlenecks.

### 4. Strategy Pruning & Fine-Tuning
- **Signal Trimming:** Merged PRs to kill underperforming strategies like `quan_engine_scalp`, throttle `ml_crypto_predictor`, and enforce an `elite_score >= 70` gate.
- **Filtering & Timings:** Implemented a Crypto Time-Of-Day (TOD) 16-21 UTC filter alongside a confidence dead-zone gate to avoid poor trading windows.
- **Symbol & Asset Enhancements:** Rolled out non-crypto TIER1/TIER2 symbol sets for the elite scorer and fixed HC forex floors to match the test contract.
