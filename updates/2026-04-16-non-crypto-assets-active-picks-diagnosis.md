# Diagnosis: Lack of Active Picks in Non-Crypto Assets & Low Performance in Forex/Equities

## 1. Missing Active Picks for Commodities, Futures, ETFs, and Bonds

The core reason these asset classes show virtually no active picks lies in how the `non_crypto_consensus.py` engine aggregates files, along with missing asset-specific pipelines.

### Diagnosis
- **Commodities & Equities**: The `copy_trader_intel/non_crypto_consensus.py` script attempts to load files using hardcoded paths that contain typos relative to what is actually generated by the scrapers.
  - It looks for `commodities_copytrader_picks.json`, but the actual generated file is `commodity_copytrader_picks.json` (singular).
  - It looks for `equity_copytrader_picks.json`, but the actual generated file is `stocks_copytrader_picks.json`.
  - Because these files cannot be found, the consensus engine loads 0 picks for them, resulting in no active consensus output.
- **Futures**: Although the file `futures_copytrader_picks.json` is successfully generated and populated by `okx_futures_scraper.py`, it is **completely missing** from the `run_consensus()` loading block in `non_crypto_consensus.py`. It is never read or extended into `all_picks`.
- **ETFs and Bonds**: There are no dedicated copy-trader JSON files (e.g., `etf_copytrader_picks.json` or `bonds_copytrader_picks.json`), nor are there specific `asset_class_analysis_bonds.py` or `asset_class_analysis_etfs.py` scripts to process them. They fall entirely outside the consensus aggregation flow.

### Suggested Plan to Fix
1. **Fix File Paths in `non_crypto_consensus.py`**:
   - Update `commodity_path` to point to `commodity_copytrader_picks.json`.
   - Update `equity_path` to point to `stocks_copytrader_picks.json`.
2. **Include Futures**:
   - Add a new block in `run_consensus()` to load `futures_copytrader_picks.json` and extend `all_picks`.
3. **Establish ETF & Bond Pipelines**:
   - Create generic scrapers or replicators for ETFs and Bonds.
   - Add integration points in `non_crypto_consensus.py` to ingest their outputs.

## 2. Low Win Rate / Profit Factor in Forex and Equities (Stocks)

According to the analysis files (`forex_asset_analysis.json` and `equity_asset_analysis.json`), both asset classes suffer from distinct mechanical trading flaws.

### Forex Diagnosis
The primary issue is a **0.0% close rate** (0 out of 19 analyzed picks have closed), which directly torpedoes the win rate and profit factor because trades are left floating indefinitely or stopped out unfavorably.
- **Missing Expirations & Loose Stops**: Picks lack max hold time expirations and use loose TP/SL ratios.
- **Mean-Reversion Timing**: Forex mean-reversion trades are attempting to trigger on fast timeframes, but the data shows these require 5-20 day holds to play out.
- **Session Timing Blindness**: Trades are being opened outside of primary market active sessions (e.g., London/NY overlap), suffering from low liquidity and choppy volatility.
- **Misapplied Carry Trades**: Carry strategies are being applied to pairs with low or negative yield differentials, actively bleeding swap fees.

### Equities/Stocks Diagnosis
Equities suffer from unrealistic targets and fighting primary market trends:
- **Unrealistic TP Targets (CRITICAL)**: 27 out of 38 active picks have Take-Profit targets that are astronomically high for swing trades (e.g., MSTR 175%, MARA 96%). The system is pulling 12-month forward analyst targets from Yahoo Finance instead of calculating actionable swing trading targets.
- **Trading Against the Trend (HIGH)**: 23 picks entered LONG positions while the asset was trading below its 200-day Simple Moving Average (SMA) – fighting the long-term trend.
- **Underperforming vs SPY (MEDIUM)**: A significant portion of the picks (24) are underperforming the broader market (SPY) over a 63-day period, meaning capital is being tied up in relatively weak assets (e.g., HOOD -35%, CRM -21% RS).

### Suggested Plan to Fix
1. **Forex Pipeline Fixes**:
   - Add a hard max-hold expiration and tighter TP/SL parameters with a trailing stop (triggering at 50% of TP distance) to force pick closures.
   - Ensure mean-reversion signals require `hold_max >= 10 days`.
   - Add an entry filter to only allow execution during the active sessions (13-16 UTC) of the pair's primary market.
   - Filter carry trade signals to only allow pairs with a `yield_diff > 2%` (e.g., AUDJPY, EURJPY, GBPJPY).
2. **Equities Pipeline Fixes**:
   - Cap equity TP targets to realistic swing percentages: 15% for large-cap, 20% for mid-cap, and 25% for small-cap, overriding the 12-month analyst consensus.
   - Implement a primary trend filter: Only allow LONG entries if the current price is strictly above the 200-day SMA.
   - Implement a Relative Strength filter: Only generate LONG signals for stocks that are outperforming SPY over the past quarter (63 days).
