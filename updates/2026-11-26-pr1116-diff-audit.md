## Reading of the PR

- **Not a 894k-line review.** Reviewable surface is ~53k lines of code plus ~60k
  lines of markdown; the rest is regenerable data that should not be in git.
- **The data should not be committed at all.** `data/cache/`, `data/rates_cache/`,
  `paper_trading/live_sim/bars/`, and `docs/audit_review_*/_raw/` are caches and
  snapshots. They belong behind the existing ignore rules (same family as the
  `.pt` model leak fixed in `updates/2026-11-25-gitignore-sweep-model-commit-fix.md`).
- **It cannot merge as-is.** 193 commits, conflicted against `main`, and no
  `refs/pull/1116/merge` exists — which is also why it has never had PR CI
  (see `updates/2026-11-26-pr-checks-missing-due-to-merge-conflicts.md`).
- Recommended split: (a) code + workflow changes, ~53k lines; (b) docs/reports;
  (c) drop the data caches entirely rather than porting them.
