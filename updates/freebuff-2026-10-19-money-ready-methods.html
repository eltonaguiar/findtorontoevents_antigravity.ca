✅ Task 10 — alpha_engine/benchmark_closed_ledger.py (new)

The plan named this module; it didn't exist. It benchmarks every closed-pick ledger against (a) its same-window benchmark via a paired bootstrap (publish gate: ≥95% of resamples must beat the benchmark) and (b) a random-entry Monte-Carlo (10k shuffled entries, same hold lengths — percentile ~1.0 = timing edge, ~0.5 = coin flip). Self-contained stdlib stats core; explicit benchmark map (EQUITY/ETF→SPY, CRYPTO→BTC-USD); WR hidden below n=20; fail-soft with reasons, never exceptions.

3. The plan (as adopted today)

  1. Phase 1 — reproduce & calibrate. Done. Reference backtest reproduces within tolerance; coin-flip control correctly rejected; gates sort no-edge vs edge correctly (both Grok's harness and our own seeded tests).
  2. Phase 2 — lock it in. Freeze pilot RULES + SHA-256 (done on the combined-book branch); every published card NOT MONEY-READY; no ENTER anywhere until a class clears the gate (renderer work shipped today).
  3. Phase 3 — paper pilot, monthly. Record return, re-check hash, run gates, verdict: not-ready / ready / kill. Checkpoints: 12 months (live inside backtest range, else investigate), 24 months (kill rules start), 100 months (full go-live gate). First independent check-in: Nov 2.
  4. Phase 4 — per-asset-class tests. One most-studied strategy per class, settings chosen on pre-2015 data only, tested 2016+. Pass bars: stocks (QMJ basket beats SPY Sharpe post-2016 + beats 95% of random baskets); bonds (trend+carry beats AGG/IEF); commodities (trend+carry Sharpe>0.3 post-2016 + crisis-positive); ETFs (fees still pass); volatility (defined-risk put-write, drop ≤ its 2008/2020 worst); crypto (delta-neutral funding carry, 3y+ after-cost). Failure protocol: one documented retry only (counted in trials); then drop, or hold as low-weight diversifier judged on drawdown reduction; regime-failed strategies (like TSMOM post-2015) stay as capped crisis hedges, never marketed as edges; if everything fails, publish honest passive 60/40/risk-parity.
