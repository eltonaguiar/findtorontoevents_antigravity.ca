### 1. **Crypto**
- **Source**: Glassnode
  - **Asset Class**: CRYPTO

  - **Data Type**: On-chain metrics, sentiment analysis
  - **Update Frequency**: Daily
  - **Avoiding Future Bias**: Use timestamped metrics; analyze data post-publication.
  - **Backtest/Validation Approach**: Compare historical on-chain metrics with price movements.

### 2. **Forex**
- **Source**: COT Report (Commitments of Traders)
  - **Asset Class**: FOREX
