# Non-Crypto Active Picks — Diagnosis & Remediation Plan

**Date:** 2026-04-16  
**Status:** Diagnosis only — no code changes applied  
**Scope:** Commodities, Futures, ETFs, Bonds (zero active picks) + Forex/Equities (low win rate / profit factor)

---

## Executive Summary

Investigation of the full pick-generation pipeline reveals **five systemic issues** preventing non-crypto asset classes from producing active picks, plus **three distinct causes** of poor Forex/Equity performance. The single biggest blocker is that the winner filter in `forward_validator.py` hard-codes `allowed_asset_classes: ["crypto", "meme"]`, meaning the main scanner physically cannot emit picks for any other asset class.

---

## Part 1: Why Commodities, Futures, ETFs, and Bonds Have ZERO Active Picks

### Root Cause #1 — Winner Filter Hard-Blocks All Non-Crypto (CRITICAL)

**File:** `alpha_engine/forward_validator.py` (~L395-466)

The `WINNER_FILTER` gate specifies:
```python
"allowed_asset_classes": ["crypto", "meme"]
```

This means every commodity, futures, ETF, and bond signal generated by the scanner is **silently dropped** before it can become an active pick. The strategies run, score, and generate candidates — but the winner filter rejects them at the emission gate. This is the primary reason these asset classes show zero picks.

### Root Cause #2 — Score Booster Is Crypto-Only

**File:** `alpha_engine/score_booster.py` (~L908-970)

Three major score enrichment pathways explicitly skip non-crypto:

| Enrichment | Behavior for Non-Crypto |
|---|---|
| **MTF gate** (multi-timeframe confirmation) | `if asset_class != "CRYPTO": continue` — skipped |
| **Ensemble gate** (2-of-3 signal confirmation) | `if asset_class != "CRYPTO": continue` — skipped |
| **Family-based boosts** (copy_trader, kimi, rapid_fire, etc.) | All 7 families are crypto-focused |

The only non-crypto score boost pathway is `_verified_alpha_bonus` for a handful of audited strategies (`forex_rsi2_mean_reversion`, `stocks_rsi2_pullback`, `cftc_cot_commercial_signal`) which gives +10 points. This means non-crypto picks start with significantly lower composite scores compared to crypto picks that can accumulate +20 to +40 from boosters alone.

### Root Cause #3 — Empty Forward-Tested Sets Cap Confidence

**File:** `alpha_engine/commodities_strategies.py`

The `_COMMODITY_FORWARD_TESTED` set is **empty** (`set()`). Any commodity strategy not in this set has its confidence hard-capped at **0.72**. Since the set is empty, ALL commodity strategies are capped. This keeps raw scores low, making it harder to pass the Smart Picks minimum score floor.

### Root Cause #4 — Forward Gate Graduation Is Impossible

**File:** `alpha_engine/config.py`

Forward gate thresholds require:
- `FORWARD_GATE_MIN_TRADES = 50`
- `FORWARD_GATE_MIN_WR = 0.45`

Current closed trade counts by asset class:

| Asset Class | Closed Trades | Can Graduate? |
|---|---|---|
| Bond | ~8 | No (need 50) |
| Futures | ~5 | No (need 50) |
| ETF | ~4 | No (need 50) |
| Commodity | ~12 | No (need 50) |

These asset classes can never accumulate enough trades to pass the forward gate, which keeps them in "unvalidated" status, which further caps their confidence scores and priority.

### Root Cause #5 — High Smart Picks Score Floors for Low-Scoring Classes

**File:** `audit_trail/quality_gates.py` (~L191-221)

| Asset Class | Min Score Floor | Achievable Score Range | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commodity | 60 | 30-55 (no boosts) | Unreachable |
| Futures | 60 | 30-55 (no boosts) | Unreachable |
| ETF | 40 | 30-50 | Marginal |
| Bond | 40 | 30-45 | Marginal |

With score booster enrichment only available for crypto, commodity and futures picks cannot realistically hit their floor of 60. Even if Root Cause #1 were fixed, these picks would be filtered out by score floors.

## Part 2: Why Forex and Equities Have Low Win Rates / Profit Factor

### Issue A — Key Source Systems Are Blocked (Equity)

**File:** `audit_trail/quality_gates.py` (~L879-910)

| Blocked System | Stats | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| `stocks_competition` | 33.5% WR, -304% PnL, n=281 | Major equity pick source — now hard-blocked |
| `fast_stocks_competition` | 14.3% WR, -41% PnL, n=21 | Secondary equity source — hard-blocked |

Additionally, 4 equity strategies are individually blocked:

| Strategy | Win Rate | Sample |
|---|---|---|
| Value + Quality | 6.2% | n=48 |
| Consecutive Beats | 25.6% | n=39 |
| Earnings Drift | 15.8% | n=19 |
| Dividend Aristocrats | 0% | n=8 |

These blocks are correct (the strategies genuinely lost money), but the result is that equity is left with very few remaining strategy paths that generate picks.

### Issue B — Forex TP/SL Is Extremely Tight

**File:** `alpha_engine/config.py` (~L152)

| Asset | SL | TP | R:R |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forex | -0.2% | +0.3% | 1.5:1 |
| Crypto | -8% | +15% | 1.875:1 |
| Equity | -3% / -6% | +5% / +12% | 1.67-2.0:1 |

Forex TP/SL of -0.2%/+0.3% is extremely tight for daily-frequency data from yfinance. Normal daily forex moves are 0.3-0.8%, meaning:
- The SL gets triggered by routine noise
- The TP is at the lower end of a normal daily range

This virtually guarantees a high stop-loss hit rate. The per-strategy ATR hard caps may help, but the base CATEGORY_RISK values create a death trap for any forex pick using defaults.

### Issue C — Key Forex Strategies Are Blocked

**File:** `audit_trail/quality_gates.py` (~L1094-1118)

| Blocked for Forex | Type |
|---|---|
| `signal_validation` | Source system |
| `MomentumEMA` | Strategy |
| `volume_spike_breakout` | Strategy |
| `myfxbook_retail_contrarian` | Strategy (contradicts its PROVEN status in forward_validator) |

The `myfxbook_retail_contrarian` strategy is simultaneously listed as a `PROVEN_STRATEGY` in `forward_validator.py` and blocked in `BLOCKED_ASSET_STRATEGY_PAIRS` for FOREX — a direct contradiction.

### Issue D — No Technical Analysis Validation for Non-Crypto

**File:** `copy_trader_intel/technical_analyzer.py`

The technical analyzer filters to `asset_class == 'CRYPTO'` and `symbol.endswith('USDT')`. Non-crypto picks receive zero TA confirmation, which means:
- No RSI/MACD/BB alignment check
- No multi-timeframe trend confirmation
- Quality enhancer can't add the +0.05 TA alignment bonus
- Quality enhancer might apply -0.10 TA conflict penalty by default

### Issue E — Non-Crypto Consensus Requires Multi-Source Agreement

**File:** `copy_trader_intel/non_crypto_consensus.py`

Consensus requires ≥2 independent strategies to agree on direction for a symbol. With fewer strategy implementations and several blocked, many symbols only generate 1 signal and get dropped.

## Part 3: Remediation Plan (Prioritized)

### P0 — Unblock Non-Crypto from Winner Filter

**Change:** `alpha_engine/forward_validator.py`  
**Action:** Expand `allowed_asset_classes` to include `"forex"`, `"equity"`, `"commodity"`, `"futures"`, `"etf"`, `"bond"`  
**Risk:** Low — these asset classes already have quality gates, score floors, and consensus checks downstream  
**Impact:** Immediately allows non-crypto picks to be emitted from the main scanner pipeline

### P1 — Fix Forex TP/SL to Realistic Levels

**Change:** `alpha_engine/config.py` CATEGORY_RISK for `"forex"`  
**Action:** Widen to approximately SL: -1.0%, TP: +1.5% (or better, make ATR-based dynamic stops the default)  
**Risk:** Medium — need to validate against historical forex pick data  
**Impact:** Reduces noise stop-outs, allows forex picks to breathe

### P1 — Extend Score Booster to Non-Crypto

**Change:** `alpha_engine/score_booster.py`  
**Action:** Remove the `if asset_class != "CRYPTO": continue` guard from MTF gate and ensemble gate, or create equivalent non-crypto enrichment using yfinance multi-timeframe data  
**Risk:** Medium — need yfinance-based MTF logic (not Binance API)  
**Impact:** Non-crypto picks can earn +10 to +30 enrichment points, making score floors reachable

### P2 — Lower Forward Gate Thresholds for Non-Crypto

**Change:** `alpha_engine/config.py`  
**Action:** Add asset-class-specific forward gate thresholds — e.g., `FORWARD_GATE_MIN_TRADES_NONCRYPTO = 15` instead of 50  
**Risk:** Low — accepts more uncertainty but enables the graduation loop to start  
**Impact:** Non-crypto strategies can begin graduating, unblocking the confidence cap

### P2 — Populate `_COMMODITY_FORWARD_TESTED` Set

**Change:** `alpha_engine/commodities_strategies.py`  
**Action:** Add commodity strategies that have ≥10 closed trades with >40% WR to the forward-tested set, removing the 0.72 confidence cap  
**Risk:** Low  
**Impact:** Commodity picks score higher, can pass quality gates

### P2 — Reduce Score Floors for Commodity/Futures

**Change:** `audit_trail/quality_gates.py`  
**Action:** Lower commodity and futures Smart Picks min score from 60 to 40 (matching bond/ETF), at least until score booster enrichment is extended  
**Risk:** Low — temporary measure while enrichment gap is fixed  
**Impact:** More commodity/futures candidates pass the quality gate

### P3 — Build Non-Crypto Technical Analyzer

**Change:** `copy_trader_intel/technical_analyzer.py`  
**Action:** Add yfinance-based TA path for forex, equity, commodity, ETF, bond, futures symbols  
**Risk:** Medium — requires new data fetching logic  
**Impact:** Non-crypto picks get TA confirmation/rejection, improving quality signal

### P3 — Resolve myfxbook_retail_contrarian Contradiction

**Change:** Either `forward_validator.py` or `quality_gates.py`  
**Action:** Audit actual performance. If WR > 45%, remove from BLOCKED_ASSET_STRATEGY_PAIRS. If WR < 40%, remove from PROVEN_STRATEGIES.  
**Risk:** Low  
**Impact:** One more forex strategy available (or accurately blocked)

### P3 — Relax Non-Crypto Consensus from ≥2 to ≥1 for Thin Asset Classes

**Change:** `copy_trader_intel/non_crypto_consensus.py`  
**Action:** For commodity, futures, bond, ETF — allow single-source picks if confidence > 0.70 and score > 50  
**Risk:** Medium — trades off consensus safety for pick volume  
**Impact:** Prevents commodity/bond signals from being dropped due to insufficient multi-source coverage
