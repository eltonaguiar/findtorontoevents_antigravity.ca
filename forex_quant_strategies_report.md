# SECTION 1: BANK TRADING DESKS (GOLDMAN, CITI, JPM)

## Post-Volcker Rule Trading Landscape

### How They Trade FX Now

**Market Making vs. Prop Trading Split**
- **Volcker Rule Impact**: Prohibits proprietary trading (trading for own account unrelated to client needs)
- Banks must demonstrate trades are tied to customer activity or risk management
- Shifted from "position-taking" to "flow trading" and client facilitation
- Focus on **liquidity provision** rather than directional speculation

**Current Revenue Model (2024-2025)**
| Segment | Revenue Source | Capital Intensity |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Market Making | Bid-ask spreads, volume rebates | Medium |
| Client Facilitation | Commission, markup on risk transfer | Low-Medium |
| Prime Services | Interest on margin, financing spreads | High |
| Advisory | FX structuring for corporates | Low |

### Client Flow Exploitation Strategies

**1. Order Flow Analysis**
- Banks see aggregate client positioning before market moves
- Corporate hedging flows provide directional signals
- Real-money (pension/insurance) flows indicate long-term trends
- **Edge**: Internalize offsetting flows to minimize market impact

**2. Information Asymmetry**
- Client inquiry patterns reveal upcoming large transactions
- M&A-related FX flows anticipated weeks in advance
- Central bank intervention timing sometimes pre-announced to primary dealers

**3. Risk Transfer Pricing**
- When clients need to hedge large exposures, banks extract premium
- Stressed market conditions = wider spreads and better margins
- **Example**: During April 2025 tariff volatility, dealers internalized 70%+ of client trades

### Macro-Driven Positioning

**Current Institutional Approach (2025)**
- Central bank divergence is THE dominant theme
- Fed holding at 4.25-4.50% while ECB cuts to ~2.75%
- Long USD vs EUR structural positions common
- Carry trades funded in JPY (despite BoJ normalization)

**Risk Management Framework**
- VaR limits strictly enforced post-Volcker
- Stress testing for sudden carry trade unwinds
- Correlation breakdown scenarios (ref: August 2024 JPY rally)

**Expected Returns**

### Mean Reversion in Currencies

**Where It Works**
- Range-bound markets (EUR/CHF historically)
- Post-volatility spike exhaustion
- Central bank intervention levels

**Where It Fails**
- Trending carry trade environments
- During major policy shifts
- When VIX > 30

**Implementation**
- Bollinger Band mean reversion
- RSI extreme reversals (with trend filter)
- Statistical arbitrage on correlated pairs

- UK inflation elevated but falling

**Trading Approach**

**Institutional Edge**
- Distinguish hedging from directional flow
- OTM put buying often = hedging (bullish for underlying)
- ATM put buying = speculative bearish
- Open interest changes reveal positioning

**Key Metrics**
- 25-delta risk reversal
- Net GEX (gamma exposure)
- Dealer positioning models
- Unusual volume alerts

**Trade Signals**
- Large OTM call buying = bullish speculation
- Put/call ratio extremes = contrarian signals
- Volatility term structure steepening = event risk pricing

# ACTIONABLE FOREX QUANT STRATEGIES

## Strategy 1: Central Bank Divergence Momentum

**Concept**: Trade in direction of widening interest rate differentials

**Entry Criteria**
1. Identify central banks on divergent paths
2. Wait for post-meeting confirmation (24-48 hours)
3. Enter on pullback to 20-period moving average

**Risk Management**
- Stop: 1.5% of position
- Position size: 2% risk per trade
- Trail stop at 2x ATR

**Expected Returns**
- Win rate: 55-60%
- Average win: 2.5%
- Average loss: 1.5%
- Expectancy: +0.9% per trade

**Implementation Difficulty**: Medium

---

## Strategy 2: Carry Trade with Volatility Filter

**Concept**: Collect interest rate differential while avoiding volatility spikes

**Currency Selection**
- Long: USD, MXN, AUD (high yield)
- Short: JPY, CHF (low yield)

**Entry Criteria**
- VIX < 20 (calm environment)
- Interest differential > 3%
- Price above 200-day MA (trend confirmation)

**Exit Criteria**
- VIX > 30 (volatility spike)
- Central bank signals policy shift
- Stop loss: 5% from entry

**Expected Returns**
- Annual carry: 3-5%
- Capital appreciation: 0-5%
- Total return: 3-10% annually
- Max drawdown: 10-15% (with proper sizing)

**Implementation Difficulty**: Low-Medium

---

## Strategy 3: Mean Reversion Post-Volatility

**Concept**: Fade extreme moves after volatility spikes

**Entry Criteria**
1. Identify 2+ standard deviation move
2. Wait for VIX peak and decline
3. Enter counter-trend at key support/resistance

**Timeframe**: 1-4 weeks

**Risk Management**
- Stop: Beyond the extreme (1.5x the move)
- Target: 50% retracement of the spike
- Position size: 1% risk (high uncertainty)

**Expected Returns**
- Win rate: 45-50%
- Average win: 4%
- Average loss: 2%
- Expectancy: +0.8% per trade

**Implementation Difficulty**: Medium

---

## Strategy 4: Options Flow Momentum

**Concept**: Follow institutional options positioning

**Signals**
- Large block call purchases (OTM) = Bullish
- Put/call ratio < 0.7 = Bullish extreme (contrarian)
- Unusual volume in single strikes = Event positioning

**Entry/Exit**
- Entry: Same day as large flow
- Exit: 1-2 weeks or when flow reverses
- Stop: 1% adverse move

**Expected Returns**
- Win rate: 50-55%
- Average hold: 5 days
- Expectancy: +0.5% per trade

**Implementation Difficulty**: High (requires options data feed)

---

## Strategy 5: Cross-Asset Correlation Arbitrage

**Concept**: Exploit temporary dislocations between FX and other assets

**Setups**
1. S&P 500 vs. USD/JPY correlation breakdown
2. Gold vs. USD inverse relationship
3. Oil vs. CAD/NOK

**Entry Criteria**
- Correlation breaks below 0.5 (historical average 0.7+)
- Fundamental reason for divergence absent
- Position for correlation reversion

**Risk Management**
- Stop: When correlation continues diverging for 5+ days
- Position size: 1.5% risk
- Time stop: 2 weeks

**Expected Returns**
- Win rate: 60%
- Average win: 2%
- Average loss: 1.5%
- Expectancy: +0.75% per trade

**Implementation Difficulty**: Medium-High
