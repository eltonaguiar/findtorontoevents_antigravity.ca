Meme coins operate on reflexivity - price drives sentiment, sentiment drives buying:

1. **Early Phase**: Low price, low awareness, accumulation
2. **Momentum Phase**: Price rising, social mentions increasing
3. **Euphoria Phase**: Mainstream attention, FOMO buying
4. **Distribution Phase**: Smart money selling to retail
5. **Collapse Phase**: Price crashes, bag holders remain

**Trading Implication**: Enter in phases 1-2, exit in phase 3, never hold through phase 4-5.

## THE PSYCHOLOGY OF MEME COIN TRADING

### Why Meme Coins Work

1. **Attention Economy**: Memes spread faster than fundamentals
2. **Community Identity**: Holding becomes tribal affiliation
3. **Low Barrier to Entry**: Anyone can buy millions of tokens for $100
4. **Lottery Effect**: Small investment, massive potential payoff
5. **Social Proof**: Seeing others profit drives FOMO

### Common Psychological Traps

**FOMO (Fear of Missing Out):**
- Buying at peaks after seeing others profit
- Solution: Set entry targets in advance, stick to plan

**HODL Mentality:**
- Refusing to sell due to attachment
- Solution: Set profit-taking levels before buying

**Revenge Trading:**
- Increasing position sizes after losses
- Solution: Daily loss limits, cooling-off periods

**Confirmation Bias:**
- Only seeking information that supports position
- Solution: Actively seek bearish perspectives

### Mental Framework for Success

**The Degen Mindset:**
- Accept that 90% of meme coins will go to zero
- Treat each trade as independent probability bet
- Never invest more than you can afford to lose completely
- Focus on process (risk management) over outcomes (P&L)
