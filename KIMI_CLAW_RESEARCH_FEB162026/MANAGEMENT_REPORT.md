# RISK ASSESSMENT

## Risk Categories

### 1. Market Risk (HIGH)
**Description**: Losses due to adverse market movements

**Mitigation**:
- Multi-asset diversification (6 asset classes)
- 113 strategies reduce single-strategy risk
- Dynamic position sizing based on volatility
- Stop-losses on all positions (-8% max)

**Residual Risk**: Medium - Market crashes can correlate across strategies

### 2. Model Risk (MEDIUM)
**Description**: Strategies fail due to changing market conditions

**Mitigation**:
- Continuous backtesting and validation
- Multi-agent pipeline generates new strategies
- Walk-forward analysis required
- Out-of-sample testing mandatory

**Residual Risk**: Medium - Some decay inevitable

### 3. Operational Risk (MEDIUM)
**Description**: Execution failures, data errors, system outages

**Mitigation**:
- Multiple data source redundancy
- Cloud-based infrastructure
- Automated monitoring and alerts
- Manual override capabilities

**Residual Risk**: Low - Robust infrastructure in place

### 4. Liquidity Risk (MEDIUM)
**Description**: Inability to exit positions at desired prices

**Mitigation**:
- Position sizing based on average daily volume
- Avoid < $10M market cap without special handling
- Slippage modeling in backtests
- Gradual position building

**Residual Risk**: Medium - Penny stocks and meme coins vulnerable

### 5. Manipulation Risk (HIGH)
**Description**: Pump-and-dump schemes, market manipulation

**Mitigation**:
- 12-strategy pump protection framework
- Volume profile analysis
- Wallet distribution monitoring
- Social sentiment authenticity checks

**Residual Risk**: Medium - Cannot eliminate all manipulation risk

### 6. Regulatory Risk (MEDIUM)
**Description**: Changes in trading regulations

**Mitigation**:
- Focus on regulated markets where possible
- Compliance monitoring
- Geographic diversification
- Legal review of strategies

**Residual Risk**: Medium - Crypto regulation evolving

### 7. Technology Risk (LOW)
**Description**: Software bugs, API failures, data corruption

**Mitigation**:
- Extensive testing before deployment
- Paper trading phase (30 days minimum)
- Version control and rollback capabilities
- Daily data validation

**Residual Risk**: Low - Strong engineering practices
