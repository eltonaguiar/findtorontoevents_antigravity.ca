## PART 1: TIER 1 STRATEGIES - EDGE ANALYSIS

### 1.1 CROSS-SECTIONAL MOMENTUM (Jegadeesh-Titman)

**Where Does the Edge Come From?**
- **Behavioral underreaction**: Investors anchor to prior beliefs and underreact to new information
- **Gradual information flow**: Information diffuses slowly across investors
- **Herding behavior**: Winners attract more buyers, creating self-reinforcing trends

**Why Hasn't It Been Arbitraged Away?**
- Requires **short-selling** (costly, constrained)
- **Crash risk**: Momentum strategies experience occasional severe drawdowns (2009, 2020)
- **Capacity constraints**: Limited by liquidity of small-cap stocks where effect is strongest
- **Behavioral biases are hardwired**: Human psychology doesn't change

**Economic Rationale**
- Investors exhibit **disposition effect** (sell winners too early, hold losers too long)
- **Analyst forecast anchoring**: Slow revision of earnings estimates
- **Institutional constraints**: Mandates prevent many funds from following momentum

**Who's On The Other Side?**
- **Value investors** buying "cheap" stocks that are cheap for a reason
- **Contrarian retail traders** fighting the trend
- **Index funds** providing liquidity without directional bias
- **Corporate hedgers** selling winners to lock in gains

**Edge Durability**
| Metric | Estimate | Source |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Half-life | 10-15 years | McLean & Pontiff (2016) |
| Post-publication decay | 26-58% | Academic studies |
| Capacity | $10-50B | Liquidity constraints |
| Competition | HIGH | Widely known |

**Verdict**: ✅ **GENUINE EDGE** - But expect 30-50% lower returns than historical backtests

## PART 3: WHY POPULAR STRATEGIES DON'T WORK

### 3.1 MACD CROSSOVERS

**Why They DON'T Work**

1. **Published in 1979**: 46 years of arbitrage opportunity
2. **Every retail platform offers it**: Billions of traders watching same signal
3. **No economic rationale**: Just moving average math
4. **Lagging indicator**: Signals after move has occurred
5. **Whipsawed in ranging markets**: Loses money when markets chop

**Academic Evidence**
- Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron (1992): Found some predictive power pre-1990
- **Post-1990**: Performance degraded significantly
- Modern studies: No statistically significant alpha after transaction costs

**Who's On The Other Side?**
- **Smart money** fading retail MACD signals
- **HFT algos** front-running predictable flows
- **Market makers** collecting spread from MACD traders

**Half-life**: <2 years (already dead)

**Verdict**: ❌ **NO EDGE** - Textbook example of arbitraged indicator

---

### 3.2 RSI OVERBOUGHT/OVERSOLD

**Why They DON'T Work**

1. **Mean reversion assumption**: Markets trend more than revert
2. **No position sizing**: Binary overbought/oversold signals
3. **Divergence doesn't predict**: RSI divergence has no predictive power
4. **Self-fulfilling for a while**: Then becomes contrarian signal
5. **Ignored in strong trends**: Can stay overbought/oversold for months

**Academic Evidence**
- Han, Yang & Zhou (2013): RSI has weak predictive power
- **Out-of-sample**: Performance degrades significantly
- Transaction costs: Erase any remaining edge

**Who's On The Other Side?**
- **Momentum traders** riding trends
- **Institutional algos** fading RSI extremes
- **Trend-following CTAs** ignoring RSI

**Half-life**: <2 years (already dead)

**Verdict**: ❌ **NO EDGE** - Mean reversion rarely works in trending markets

---

### 3.3 SIMPLE MOVING AVERAGE CROSSES

**Why They DON'T Work**

1. **Most basic technical rule**: Everyone knows it
2. **Lagging by definition**: Based on past prices
3. **No economic foundation**: Just mathematical smoothing
4. **Curve-fitted parameters**: 50/200 chosen because they "look good"
5. **Whipsaw losses**: Multiple false signals in choppy markets

**Academic Evidence**
- Brock et al. (1992): Worked pre-1990
- **Post-2000**: No excess returns after costs
- Modern markets: HFT algos exploit predictable SMA flows

**Who's On The Other Side?**
- **HFT firms** detecting SMA signals and fading them
- **Quant funds** with faster trend detection
- **Smart money** knowing retail stops are at SMAs

**Half-life**: <2 years (already dead)

**Verdict**: ❌ **NO EDGE** - The most arbitraged technical rule

---

### 3.4 BOLLINGER BAND BOUNCES

**Why They DON'T Work**

2. **Standard deviation doesn't predict**: Statistical measure, not predictive
3. **False security**: 95% of prices within bands doesn't mean edge
4. **Trending market killer**: Bounces fail in strong trends
5. **Over-optimized**: Band width parameters curve-fitted

**Who's On The Other Side?**
- **Trend followers** riding breakouts
- **Momentum algos** breaking through bands
- **Institutional flow** ignoring technical levels

**Verdict**: ❌ **NO EDGE** - Mean reversion rarely pays
