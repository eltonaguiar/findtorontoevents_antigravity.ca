## Slide 2: The Sharpe Ratio Edge

### What is Sharpe Ratio?

```
Sharpe Ratio = (Return - Risk-Free Rate) ÷ Volatility
```

| Sharpe Ratio | Interpretation |
|--------------|----------------|
| < 0.5 | Poor risk-adjusted returns |
| 0.5 - 1.0 | Acceptable |
| 1.0 - 1.5 | Good |
| 1.5 - 2.0 | Excellent |
| > 2.0 | Exceptional |

**Our Target**: Stocks with 3-year Sharpe > 1.0
