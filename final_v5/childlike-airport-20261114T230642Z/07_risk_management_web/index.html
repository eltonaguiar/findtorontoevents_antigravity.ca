1. Position & Exposure Limits

  • Risk per trade: ≤ 1–2% of risk capital (hard cap).
  • Sizing: fractional Kelly (≤ 0.25–0.5×) only for edges passing gates; else fixed-fraction/vol-target.
  • Concentration: cap per symbol/strategy/asset-class and correlated cluster; diversify sleeves.
  • Leverage: default none; if used, pre-defined max gross/net and margin buffer.

2. Loss & Drawdown Controls

  • Stop loss: strategy-defined, gap-aware; assume slippage/worse fills (no optimistic fills).
  • Daily loss cap: halt new risk after −3% to −5% (configure per sleeve).
  • Monthly drawdown cap: halt & review at −10% to −20%; flatten to baseline on breach.
  • Losing streak: pause after 3 consecutive full-risk losses; require post-mortem.

3. Validation & Promotion Gates

  • Clean/provenanced data; reconciled PnL (gross & net); fresh marks (no silent 0%).
  • Net-of-realistic costs, OOS/walk-forward, benchmark-relative, leak-safe.
  • Targets: PF≥1.5 (≥1.2 contextual), Sharpe≥1.0, MDD<20% (prefer <15%), PBO<0.3, DSR>0.95 (effective-N), n≥100 (≥500 pref. crypto).
  • Independent reproduction + monitored paper-forward before any capital.

4. Data, QA & Kill-Switch

  • 0% PnL / blanks: every open & recently-closed pick must be dispositioned (validated vs suspect/stale/unquoted) against market data.
  • Freshness SLA: no new risk on stale marks/feeds; surface UNQUOTED/STALE states.
  • Auto kill-switch triggers: gate failure 2 checks, DD breach, stale data, unresolved PnL/mark defect, regime/vol shock beyond mandate.
  • Manual halt: on unexplained reconciliation drift or incident.

6. Operational Checklist (pre-trade / ongoing)

  • ✔ Cohort reconciled; critical fields non-blank; provenance & timestamps valid.
  • ✔ 0% PnL picks dispositioned with fresh market-data comparison.
  • ✔ Gross & net metrics computed; costs/slippage/funding modeled per class.
  • ✔ Regime/freshness gates green; benchmarks loaded for same window.
  • ▲ Paper-forward metrics within tolerance; drift monitored.
  • ✖ Any unresolved integrity defect, stale feed, or gate breach → no new risk.
