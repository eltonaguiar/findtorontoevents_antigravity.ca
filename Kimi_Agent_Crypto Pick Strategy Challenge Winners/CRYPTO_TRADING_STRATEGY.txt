SECTION 5: TRADING RULES & CHECKLIST
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRE-TRADE CHECKLIST:
☐ Confirm trend with Golden Cross (50/200 SMA)
☐ Check Z-Score for overbought/oversold conditions
☐ Verify price at Bollinger Band extremes
☐ Calculate ATR for position sizing
☐ Set Stop Loss at 1.5x ATR
☐ Set Take Profit at mean reversion target
☐ Confirm Risk/Reward ratio >= 1:1
☐ Risk only 1-2% of account per trade

ENTRY RULES:
• LONG:  Z-Score < -1.5 AND Price < BB Lower
• SHORT: Z-Score > +1.5 AND Price > BB Upper

EXIT RULES:
• Take Profit 1: Bollinger Band Middle (20 SMA)
• Take Profit 2: 50 SMA (partial close)
• Stop Loss: Breakeven once TP1 reached
