How It Works — Algorithm Battle Royale

91 Competing Algorithms: Including MACD, RSI, Bollinger, StochRSI, EMA crossovers, volume spike detectors, Deribit options flow (PCR), crypto funding rate sentiment, Fear & Greed Index, and on-chain signals.

Elimination Engine: Algorithms that underperform enter a danger zone, then probation, then elimination. Top 20 challengers compete for the eliminated slots — only the fittest survive.

Pump Detection: Volume spike analysis identifies unusual buying activity. Candidates are scored by magnitude, consistency, and alignment with technical signals across multiple timeframes.

Self-Adjusting ML Ranker: A heuristic ranker scores algorithms by win rate, profit factor, and consistency. At 50+ resolved picks, it auto-trains a Random Forest model to rank algorithms by expected performance.

Rug Pull Guard: Volatility analysis flags tokens with abnormal price/volume patterns that may indicate manipulation, protecting against pump-and-dump schemes.
