How It Works — Self-Learning ML System

8 Technical Strategies: MACD Crossover, Bollinger Squeeze, RSI Extremes, StochRSI+MACD Confluence, Volume Spike, EMA Stack Alignment, VWAP Reversion, Mean Reversion.

Self-Learning Weights: Each strategy's confidence is dynamically adjusted based on its historical win rate. Strategies that perform well get boosted (up to 1.5x), underperformers get dampened (down to 0.5x). Weights retrain automatically from resolved picks stored in MySQL.

Signal Pipeline: Binance 1h klines → technical indicator computation → strategy rule evaluation → confidence scoring with adaptive weights → ATR-based TP/SL (1.5:1 and 2:1 R:R) → pick output with auto-resolution tracking.

Resolution: Every 15-minute scan resolves prior picks against live prices. Wins/losses feed back into strategy weights, creating a continuously improving system.
