## New Strategies Created

### 1. `forex_mean_reversion_divergence.py`
**Asset Class**: FOREX (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCHF, USDCAD, EURJPY, GBPJPY)  
**Problem**: 28.6% WR on 7 picks — almost no forex coverage  
**Logic**: Connors RSI(2) extreme + Bollinger Band touch + volume spike + London/NY session filter + ADX < 25 (ranging markets only)  
**Target**: WR 58-65%, PF > 1.5, picks/day 3-8  
**Key Innovation**: Session filter (08:00-17:00 UTC) ensures liquidity; ADX filter avoids trading in trends where mean-reversion fails  
**Monte Carlo**: Built-in permutation test (1000 simulations)

### 2. `commodity_seasonal_momentum.py`
**Asset Class**: COMMODITIES (GC, SI, CL, NG, ZC, ZS, HG, PL)  
**Problem**: 43.6% WR, 4 picks — underperforming  
**Logic**: Donchian(55) channel breakout + seasonal month-based bias + ADX > 20 (trending) + RSI confirmation  
**Seasonal Bias Maps**: Pre-computed monthly bias for 8 commodities (e.g., NG bullish Oct-Mar for heating season, ZC bullish Mar-Jun for planting)  
**Target**: WR 55-62%, PF > 1.5, picks/day 1-4  
**Key Innovation**: Combines seasonal patterns (well-documented edge) with momentum breakout for confirmation

### 3. `equity_earnings_drift_momentum.py`
**Asset Class**: EQUITY (30 S&P 500 stocks + ETFs)  
**Problem**: 70.8% WR but only 5 picks — needs massive scale  
**Logic**: Post-earnings announcement drift (PEAD) — one of the most documented market anomalies. Stocks beating earnings estimates drift up for 60-90 days.  
**Entry**: Earnings surprise > 10% + price > EMA(20) + 20d return > 5% + RSI < 70 + RVOL > 1.5  
**Fallback**: Momentum-only mode when earnings data unavailable  
**Target**: WR 62-70%, PF > 1.6, picks/day 5-15  
**Key Innovation**: ATR trailing stop (3x) with 1.5x TP multiplier for asymmetric risk-reward

### 4. `cross_asset_short_edge.py`
**Asset Class**: ALL (Crypto, Equity, Forex, Commodities)  
**Problem**: SHORT direction has +16% WR edge but only 23.4% of picks are SHORT  
**Logic**: Asset-class-specific SHORT signal generators exploiting:
- Crypto: Funding rate > 0.03% (overleveraged longs) + overbought RSI
- Equity: 20-day low break + sector weakness (SPY < EMA50)
- Forex: Keltner upper + RSI(2) > 90 + DXY strengthening
- Commodity: Seasonal bearish + CCI > 100 + below SMA(100)
**Target**: WR 58-65% on SHORT positions  
**Key Innovation**: Configurable min_short_allocation (default 50%) to rebalance the LONG-heavy portfolio

### 5. `crypto_adaptive_regime.py` (CARS)
**Asset Class**: CRYPTO (BTC, ETH, SOL, AVAX, LINK, etc.)  
**Problem**: 46.9% WR, over-trading SCALP mode  
**Logic**: Regime-switching approach:
- BULL regime: Pullback-to-EMA entries (LONG only)
- BEAR regime: Bounce-to-EMA entries (SHORT only)
- RANGE regime: Bollinger mean-reversion (both directions)
**Regime Detection**: SMA(200) + ADX(14) + MACD  
**Target**: WR 55-62%, PF > 1.4  
**Key Innovation**: Regime breakdown in backtest results shows which regime produces edge

### 6. `etf_sector_rotation_rs.py`

**Target**: WR 60-68%, PF > 1.5  
**Key Innovation**: Buys dips in outperforming sectors (momentum + mean-reversion hybrid)

### 7. `cross_asset_inverse.py`

**Evidence**: `claude_gainer_ml` inverse = 80% WR, PF 19.56  
**Key Innovation**: Reusable transformer that any strategy can be piped through
