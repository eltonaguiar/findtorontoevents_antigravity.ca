## Part 2: New Strategies Created

### Strategy 1: `futures_mean_reversion_rsi_bb`
**File:** `mimo_strategies/futures_mean_reversion_rsi_bb.py`
**Asset:** FUTURES | **Type:** Mean Reversion | **Timeframe:** 1d

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| RSI(2) oversold | <10 | Extreme oversold on short RSI — high reversion probability |
| BB(20,2) touch | Required | Price at statistical extreme |
| SMA(200) filter | Required for LONG | Only buy dips in uptrends |
| ATR stop | 2x ATR | Tight enough to preserve capital |
| ATR target | 3x ATR | 1.5:1 R:R minimum |

**Why futures mean reversion works:** Futures oscillate around fair value due to arbitrage with spot markets. The existing 6.3% WR strategies likely use trend-following on choppy futures — wrong approach. Mean reversion is the correct paradigm.

**Backtest requirements per TESTING_PROTOCOL.md:**
- 5yr daily, 70/15/15 walk-forward split
- Monte Carlo: 10,000 paths, 95% CI
- Transaction costs: $2.50/contract + 1 tick slippage
- Regime test: must work in ≥2 of 3 regimes (bull/bear/sideways)
