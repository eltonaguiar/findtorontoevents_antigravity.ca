## Mandatory handoff checklist

- Exact class/exposure, wrapper/contract, venue, currency and registered holding horizon.
- `spec_id`, canonical hypothesis if actually registered, family/parent ID, owner, branch/source SHA, data/spec hashes and cutoff.
- Available-at decision and first eligible fill; all missing/ambiguous states explicit.
- Matched total-return SPY/buyhold/cash/risk controls, not just WR or absolute profit.
- Net portfolio curve, drawdown/recovery, costs/capacity and uncertainty with effective evidence.
- Every attempted variant/failed run, source limits and objective-specific decision.
- Production caller/workflow and rollback only for a separately approved wiring change. This folder does not establish one.
