# Extensions and Corrections to the Prior Analysis

## What the Prior Analysis Got Right
- No class is proven profitable under the strict gates used.
- EQUITY is the best research priority for seeking alpha.
- Low-cost ETFs and mutual funds are the highest-probability path for positive real returns with lower risk — but this is beta + cost discipline, not prediction edge.
- Measurement integrity and strict promotion gates are non-negotiable.

## What Was Missing or Under-emphasized
1. **Explicit vs buy-and-hold comparison**  
   Every strategy must be judged against the relevant passive benchmark (SPY total return, BTC buy-hold, etc.). Absolute profitability is not enough if it underperforms simple holding.

2. **Mutual funds as long-horizon winners**  
   Given enough time (10+ years), low-cost diversified equity mutual funds and ETFs have high probability of positive real returns because of the equity risk premium. This does not require the prediction system to work. The system can still be used to research active overlays, but the core should not wait for alpha.

3. **Style and holding-period lens**  
   Longer holding periods dramatically reduce the cost hurdle and the difficulty of finding edge. Most of the system's historical pain comes from higher-frequency attempts.

4. **Prediction markets are structurally different**  
   Zero-sum nature + bot dominance explains the repeated failure of copy-trading approaches.

5. **Famous systems need the same standards**  
   Hoffman, TradingView contest winners, and YouTube backtests must pass the same cost-adjusted, OOS, vs-benchmark tests as internal strategies.

## Updated Priority Order
1. Measurement and cost integrity.
2. EQUITY pre-registered test vs SPY total return.
3. Parallel deployment of low-cost ETF/MF core portfolio (does not depend on alpha).
4. CRYPTO only after data/fill fixes + clean forward test that beats BTC hold.
5. Commodity CTA-style tests (historically one of the stronger active approaches).
6. Everything else remains research-only until gates clear.
