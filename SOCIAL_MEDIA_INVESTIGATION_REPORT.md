### Verification Process

1. **Find traders** posting results
2. **Check evidence** quality (screenshots, videos)
3. **Verify authenticity** (detect fakes, Photoshop)
4. **Cross-reference** market data
5. **Reverse engineer** strategy rules
6. **Backtest** with realistic costs
7. **Validate** robustness

### Red Flags We Watch For

- Only posting wins, never losses
- No broker screenshots
- Selling courses/signals
- Unrealistic returns (>100% monthly)
- Vague strategy descriptions
- "DM for signals"
- Affiliate links only

### Green Flags We Look For

- Broker screenshots with timestamps
- Video walkthroughs
- Third-party verification (Kinfo, TraderSync)
- Transparent about losses
- Specific entry/exit rules
- Risk management explained
- 6+ months of history
