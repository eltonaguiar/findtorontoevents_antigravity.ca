## What this actually establishes

**1. Cost, not signal, is the binding constraint almost everywhere.**
ETF gross edge +0.76 bps → net −9.24 bps. Coinbase premium gross +9.85 bps → net
−20.15 bps. Futures TSMOM gross +1.00 bps → net −9.00 bps. The gross edges are
real but one to two orders of magnitude smaller than round-trip cost. Funding
arb is the clearest case: the harness *passed* on sign, and cost alone killed it
(5.05% of gross survives).

**2. Win rates cluster at ~50% gross and fall below it net.**
ETF 50.31% → 45.44%. Equity momentum 50.69% → 50.45%. Coinbase premium 49.62% →
45.69%. Futures 51.44% → 44.40%. A 50% win rate cannot pay a cost. This is the
same shape across completely different mechanisms, which is what a "no edge"
population looks like.

**3. Sign instability, not low mean, is the recurring failure.**
BOND: 140 positive vs 187 negative strong windows. Coinbase premium: 26 vs 25.
Futures: 40 vs 56. The harness requires three same-sign windows. A mechanism
whose sign flips is a regime bet, not an edge.

**4. The one non-killed equity result is a data gap, not a success.**
H-033 equity momentum produced net +1.49 bps with a *passing* cost gate (66.5%
survival) but zero scorable windows. It is recorded `INSUFFICIENT_N` with the
gate named, not promoted. It is the only thing worth re-running with more data.

**5. The previous sweep's ETF PF of 1.4256 at 2x cost did not replicate.**
The picks-table cell looked best-in-class. The dedicated harness on 17,160
records found a negative net edge. The difference is that the picks cell was 79
rows dominated by a few symbols, while ET-1 measures the actual flow signal.
The apparent winner was sample size plus concentration.

## Data blockers, precisely

- `data/earnings/<TICKER>/latest.json` — 4 quarters, single `eps_estimate` snapshot
  (`fetched_at` 2026-11-19). Blocks PEAD. Needs vintage consensus.
- `data/forex_rate_cache/`, `data/commodity_price_cache/`, `data/cftc_cot/` — empty.
- `data/funding_rate_archive/` — 5 symbols (BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP).
- `data/universe/sp500_tickers.txt` — current constituents only; survivorship-biased.

## What would change the answer

Only forward rows can. The promotion rule needs n ≥ 100 forward rows dated after
registration, with intrabar-honest first touch, net-PF CI lower bound > 1.0, and
selection alpha CI lower bound > 0. No class has any of that. The honest next
step is not a new backtest variant; it is either (a) the point-in-time data that
unblocks EQUITY/FOREX/COMMODITY, or (b) accepting the kill.

## Addendum: INDEX leak and MEME skew (measured after the first commit)

**INDEX denominator leak CONFIRMED.** Five distinct symbols are filed under
`INDEX`, but across all classes those same five symbols carry 88 rows:

| category filed | rows | symbols |
|---|---|---|
| futures | 48 | 4 |
| index | 25 | 5 |
| crypto | 14 | 3 |
| equity | 1 | 1 |

So 71 of 88 rows (81%) on INDEX symbols are counted in some *other* class's
denominator. Any INDEX statistic is therefore a fragment of a population that is
double-counted elsewhere. Verdict stays `INSUFFICIENT_N`, with the leak recorded
as the reason rather than n=25 alone.

**MEME is negatively skewed, so it is a kill, not a lottery ticket.**

    n=102  mean=-0.193  median=0.000  skew=-2.356  worst=-75.00  best=+52.57

The mean is below the median, which is the opposite of the right tail a
lottery-style edge would need. One −75% loss dwarfs the best winner. Per the
methodology's step 3 ("check the shape, not just the mean"), a negative-skew
population with a zero median is not an edge. Verdict raised from `WEAK` to
`KILL`.
