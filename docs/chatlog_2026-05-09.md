### Strategy A — `sentiment_macro_contrarian` (330 LoC)

- **File:** `alpha_engine/sentiment_macro_contrarian.py`
- **Asset classes:** CRYPTO + EQUITY/ETF
- **Logic:**
  - CRYPTO: BUY on FGI < 25 + macro.usd != strong (weak USD = risk-on)
  - EQUITY/ETF: BUY on VIX > 25 + curve != inverted; SHORT on VIX < 12 + low vol + inverted curve (late-cycle complacency)
  - Confidence boost when sentiment AND macro both align
- **Backtest (synthetic):** 3 sigs (2 fear-buy, 1 complacency-short, 0 greed by design); WR estimate 0.381 = random-walk floor
- **Refs:** Whaley 2009, Estrella-Mishkin 1998, Adrian-Estrella-Shin 2010, Liu-Tsyvinski 2018, Bekaert-Hoerova 2014
- **Rollback:** `SENTIMENT_MACRO_DISABLED=1`

### Strategy B — `crypto_pairs_arb` (438 LoC)

- **File:** `alpha_engine/crypto_pairs_arb.py`
- **Asset class:** CRYPTO
- **Logic:**
  - Cointegration on BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT, ETHUSDT/SOLUSDT, BTCUSDT/BNBUSDT
  - Hedge ratio via OLS rolling 60-bar (numpy.linalg.lstsq, no statsmodels)
  - AR(1) half-life filter (skip pairs with half-life > 30 bars)
  - Mean-reversion: |z| > 2.0 fires, exit on z=0
  - 2 signal dicts per fired pair sharing pair_id; conf scales with |z|/4 capped 0.95
- **Backtest (synthetic, 365d cointegrated synthetic prices, walk-forward 180/185):** n=13, WR 0.92, Sharpe 25.3 (synth-construction floor; live expectation 55-65% per Vidyamurthy/Gatev)
- **Refs:** Engle-Granger, Vidyamurthy, Gatev
- **Rollback:** `CRYPTO_PAIRS_ARB_DISABLED=1`

### Strategy C — `regime_filtered_momentum` (362 LoC)

- **File:** `alpha_engine/regime_filtered_momentum.py`
- **Asset classes:** EQUITY + ETF
- **Logic:**
  - Asness 12-1 momentum: `(close[-21] / close[-252]) - 1`
  - Universe: TIER1_EQUITY + TIER1_ETF
  - LONG when curve in {flat, steep} + vol != elevated
  - SHORT when curve == inverted + vol == elevated
  - Top 5 LONG / Bottom 5 SHORT after regime filter; |mom| > 5% threshold
  - Graceful FRED-unavailable fallback (default-permissive LONG-only)
- **Backtest (synthetic 5×600d, 4-regime rotation):** 15 rebalances, 27 signals, hit_rate 40.7%, monthly mean +0.17%, eqweight Sharpe 0.236
- **Refs:** Asness/Frazzini/Pedersen, Estrella-Hardouvelis curve-yield
- **Rollback:** `REGIME_MOMENTUM_DISABLED=1`
