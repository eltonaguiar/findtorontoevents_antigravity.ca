### Performance by Strategy Type
| Strategy Type | Win Rate | Best Timeframe |
|--------------|----------|----------------|
| Reversal (Counter-Trend) | 62% | 4H, Daily |
| Continuation (With-Trend) | **70%** | 1H, 4H |

### The 50% Retracement Entry
- Place buy order at 50% of pin bar range
- Substantially improves risk-reward ratio
- Allows tighter stops

### Confluence Factors (Ranked by Impact)
1. **Support/Resistance Levels** - Highest impact
2. **Trendlines** - Strong confirmation
3. **Moving Averages** (50/200) - Trend alignment
4. **Fibonacci Levels** (61.8%, 78.6%) - Precision entries

### FXOpen Backtest (2015-2020)
Pin bars at **key psychological levels** (like 1.2000 in EUR/USD) had a **15-20% higher success rate** than those in ranging markets.
