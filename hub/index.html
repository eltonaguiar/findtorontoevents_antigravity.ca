Machine Learning Systems — Plain English Guide

This hub aggregates 12+ trading systems. Here's what the ML terms mean:

Term What It Means (Simple)
XGBoost Hundreds of tiny decision-makers vote together. Like asking 200 people and going with majority. Used by Mercury 2 (3 models) and ML Battleground.
LightGBM Similar to XGBoost but faster. Builds decision trees one leaf at a time. Used by Crypto ML Edge (16 per-coin models) and Mercury 2 (gainer predictor).
Random Forest 100 separate decision trees, each trained on random slices of data, then vote. Like a jury with 100 members. Used by KIMI, Claude Gainer, Alpha Engine.
GRU Neural Net Artificial brain that reads price history in sequence (like reading a story). Remembers important moments, forgets noise. Used by ML Battleground System C.
DSR Gate Deflated Sharpe Ratio — a statistical test asking "is this model's performance real or just luck?" Models must pass to generate picks.
Heuristic mode Rule-based fallback when ML hasn't trained yet. Uses simple formulas (win rate, Sharpe) instead of a trained model. Upgrades to ML automatically.
Walk-forward Training on past data, testing on future data the model never saw. Prevents "memorizing" history. Like studying chapters 1-8 then taking a test on chapter 9.
