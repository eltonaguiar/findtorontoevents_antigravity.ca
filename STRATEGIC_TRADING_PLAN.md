## SECTION 4: RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

### 4.1 POSITION SIZING ACROSS STRATEGIES

**Kelly Criterion Approach** (Fractional for safety):
```
Position Size = (Win Rate × Avg Win - Loss Rate × Avg Loss) / Avg Win
```

**Applied to Strategy Allocation**:
| Strategy | Win Rate | Avg Win | Avg Loss | Kelly % | Fractional (1/4 Kelly) |
|----------|----------|---------|----------|---------|----------------------|
| Momentum | 60% | 3% | 2% | 30% | 7.5% |
| Value | 55% | 4% | 2% | 35% | 8.75% |
| CTA | 45% | 5% | 2% | 32.5% | 8.1% |
| Mean Rev | 65% | 2% | 1.5% | 43% | 10.8% |

**Maximum Position Limits**:
- Single strategy: 15% of portfolio
- Single asset: 5% of portfolio
- Single trade: 2% of portfolio
- Correlated strategies combined: 25% of portfolio

### 4.2 DRAWDOWN CONTROL PROTOCOL

**Three-Line Defense**:

**Line 1: Soft Limit (10% drawdown)**
- Reduce position sizes by 25%
- Increase cash allocation to 20%
- Review strategy performance

**Line 2: Hard Limit (20% drawdown)**
- Reduce position sizes by 50%
- Increase cash allocation to 40%
- Pause new strategy deployments
- Activate tail risk hedges

**Line 3: Circuit Breaker (30% drawdown)**
- Reduce to 25% exposure maximum
- Activate all hedges
- Mandatory strategy review
- Consider complete de-risking

### 4.3 TAIL RISK HEDGING

**Hedge Allocation (5-10% of portfolio)**:
| Hedge Type | Cost | Protection | When to Use |
|------------|------|------------|-------------|
