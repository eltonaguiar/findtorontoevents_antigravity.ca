#### **Key Metrics**:
1. **Whale Wallet Tracking**:
   - Wallets holding >1,000 BTC ("whales")
   - Wallets holding >10,000 BTC ("mega whales")
   - Exchange wallets vs. non-exchange wallets

2. **Supply Distribution**:
   - Illiquid Supply: Coins not moved in >1 year
   - Liquid Supply: Active trading supply
   - Exchange Reserves: BTC held on exchanges

3. **Flow Indicators**:
   - Exchange Inflows: Often signals selling pressure
   - Exchange Outflows: Often signals accumulation
   - Netflow: Difference between inflows and outflows

4. **UTXO Analysis**:
   - Age bands: Distribution by last movement
   - Realized Price: Average cost basis of moved coins
   - NUPL (Net Unrealized Profit/Loss): Market sentiment indicator

## CRYPTO-SPECIFIC EDGES & ON-CHAIN SIGNALS SUMMARY

### **Alpha Sources**:

1. **On-Chain Flows**:
   - Exchange outflows = Bullish (accumulation)
   - Exchange inflows = Bearish (selling pressure)
   - Whale accumulation = Smart money positioning

2. **Derivatives Metrics**:
   - Funding rates > 0.01% = Overheated longs
   - Negative funding = Potential bottom signal
   - Open interest spikes = Volatility incoming
   - Liquidation clusters = Support/resistance levels

3. **Network Fundamentals**:
   - Hash rate trends = Security and miner health
   - Active addresses = Usage and adoption
   - Transaction fees = Network demand
   - Supply distribution = Concentration risk

4. **MEV Opportunities**:
   - Sandwich attacks = Extractable value from AMMs
   - Liquidation hunting = Profitable liquidations
   - Arbitrage = Cross-DEX price differences
   - JIT liquidity = Just-in-time LP provisioning

5. **Macro Correlations**:
   - DXY inverse correlation
   - Gold correlation during risk-off
   - Tech stock correlation (BTC as risk asset)
   - Fed policy impact on liquidity

### **Risk Management in Crypto**:

1. **Volatility Targeting**: Size positions based on realized volatility
2. **Correlation Breakdown**: Assets become correlated during crashes
3. **Liquidity Risk**: Altcoins can become illiquid quickly
4. **Custody Risk**: Not your keys, not your coins
5. **Smart Contract Risk**: Code vulnerabilities can drain funds
6. **Regulatory Risk**: Sudden policy changes can impact markets

### **Regulatory Landscape (2025-2026)**:

1. **United States**:
   - Spot Bitcoin ETFs approved (Jan 2024)
   - Ethereum ETFs approved (2024)
   - Project Crypto (July 2025) - regulatory harmonization effort
   - CLARITY Act pending - market structure framework

2. **European Union**:
   - MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets) fully implemented
   - Stablecoin regulations in effect
   - Exchange licensing requirements

3. **Asia**:
   - Singapore: Progressive licensing framework
   - Hong Kong: Retail crypto access approved
   - Japan: Established regulatory clarity
   - China: Continued ban on crypto trading
