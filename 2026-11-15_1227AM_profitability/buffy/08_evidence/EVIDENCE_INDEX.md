## 6. Known limitations of this measurement

1. **Verified ≠ intrabar-validated.** 3,618 marks are marked, not replayed. Historical replay
   reclassified ~26% of `TP_HIT` to `SL_HIT` — so PF may fall further.
2. **Three CRYPTO PFs are in circulation** (0.258 dashboard / 0.74 peer / 1.02 here) on different
   cohorts. None is authoritative until the cohort definition is pinned.
3. **Row parsing is payload-shape dependent.** `asset_class_measurability.py` walks pick-shaped dicts;
   MEME (n=10) and PENNY (n=4) appear in `asset_class_health` but their closed rows were not all
   parseable from the row walk, so they are reported from the health block only.
4. **No fill model.** All cost figures are assumptions.
