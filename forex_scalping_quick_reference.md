## Risk Management Rules

- **Never risk more than 1% per trade**
- **Max 3% daily loss limit**
- **Stop trading after 3 consecutive losses**
- **Target minimum 1:1.5 risk-reward**
- **Use ECN broker with <1 pip spread**

## Position Size Calculator

```
Lots = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Stop Loss × Pip Value)

Example: $1,000 account, 1% risk, 10 pip stop
Lots = ($1,000 × 0.01) ÷ (10 × $0.10) = 10 micro lots
```
