## Part 2: Hedge Fund Strategies That Outperform

### Category 1: Momentum & Trend Following

#### Time-Series Momentum (Moskowitz-Grinblatt-Pedersen)
- **Returns:** 10-15% annualized excess returns
- **Sharpe:** 0.8-1.0
- **Method:** Go long assets with positive 12-month returns, short negative
- **Asset Classes:** Equities, bonds, commodities, currencies
- **Key Insight:** Trend persistence across all asset classes

#### Cross-Sectional Momentum (Jegadeesh-Titman)
- **Returns:** 12-15% annualized
- **Sharpe:** 0.6-0.8
- **Method:** Buy winners (top decile), sell losers (bottom decile)
- **Holding Period:** 3-12 months
- **Key Insight:** Behavioral biases cause momentum

#### Dual Momentum (Antonacci)
- **Returns:** 12-18% annualized
- **Sharpe:** 0.9-1.2
- **Method:** Combine absolute momentum (trend) + relative momentum (strength)
- **Asset Classes:** Rotate between equities, bonds, REITs, commodities

### Category 2: Risk Parity & Multi-Asset

#### Risk Parity (Dalio/Bridgewater)
- **Returns:** 8-15% (with leverage 1.5-2x)
- **Sharpe:** 0.8-1.2
- **Method:** Balance risk contribution, not capital allocation
- **Typical Allocation:** 30% stocks, 55% bonds, 15% commodities/gold
- **Key Insight:** Bonds have lower volatility, need more capital to equalize risk

#### All-Weather Portfolio
- **Returns:** 4.7-8.4% (without leverage)
- **Sharpe:** 0.34-0.63
- **Four Economic Regimes:**
  - Rising Growth: Equities perform
  - Falling Growth: Bonds perform
  - Rising Inflation: Commodities/Gold perform
  - Falling Inflation: All assets can perform

### Category 3: Factor Investing

#### Value + Momentum + Carry
- **Value:** Buy cheap assets (low P/E, high book-to-market)
- **Momentum:** Buy trending assets
- **Carry:** Capture yield differentials (high yield bonds, currency carry)
- **Combined Sharpe:** 1.0-1.5

#### Fama-French 5-Factor Model
1. Market risk (beta)
2. Size (SMB - small minus big)
3. Value (HML - high minus low book-to-market)
4. Profitability (RMW - robust minus weak)
5. Investment (CMA - conservative minus aggressive)

## Part 3: Proven Retail-Implementable Strategies

### Strategy 1: Accelerating Dual Momentum (ADM)

**Performance:**
- CAGR: 12-18%
- Sharpe: 0.9-1.2
- Max DD: -15-25%

**Method:**
1. Calculate 12-month momentum for each asset
2. Only invest if absolute momentum is positive (above cash)
3. Select top 3 assets by relative momentum
4. Accelerate allocation to best performers

**Asset Universe:**
- US Stocks (SPY)
- International Stocks (VEU)
- US Bonds (AGG)
- REITs (VNQ)
- Commodities (DBC)
- Gold (GLD)

### Strategy 2: Risk-Managed Momentum (RMM)

**Performance:**
- CAGR: 10-15%
- Sharpe: 1.0-1.4
- Max DD: -10-18%

**Method:**
1. Identify assets in uptrend (price > 10-month MA)
2. Rank by volatility-adjusted momentum
3. Allocate using inverse volatility weighting
4. Move to cash/treasuries when trend turns negative

### Strategy 3: Factor Rotation Strategy

**Performance:**
- CAGR: 12-20%
- Sharpe: 0.8-1.2
- Max DD: -15-30%

**Method:**
1. Define factor ETFs: Value (VTV), Growth (VUG), Momentum (MTUM), Quality (QUAL)
2. Rank by 6-12 month momentum
3. Rotate into top 2 factors
4. Rebalance monthly

### Strategy 4: Multi-Asset Trend Following

**Performance:**
- CAGR: 10-18%
- Sharpe: 0.8-1.3
- Max DD: -12-20%

**Method:**
1. Monitor 20+ asset classes across equities, bonds, commodities, FX
2. Apply trend filter (e.g., price > 200-day MA)
3. Risk-adjust position sizes
4. Diversify across uncorrelated trends

### Strategy 5: Protective Asset Allocation (PAA)

**Performance:**
- CAGR: 8-14%
- Sharpe: 1.0-1.5
- Max DD: -8-15%

**Method:**
1. Define "canary" assets (risk indicators)
2. If canaries are positive: invest in risky assets
3. If canaries turn negative: move to protective assets
4. Use momentum to select within categories

## Part 4: Key Success Factors

### 1. Diversification Across Return Streams

Ray Dalio's "Holy Grail": 15 uncorrelated return streams
- Risk reduced by 80%
- Return maintained
- Sharpe ratio improves dramatically

### 2. Risk Management is Everything

**Half-Kelly Criterion:**
- Position size = (p*b - q) / b / 2
- Prevents ruin
- Allows compounding

**Maximum Drawdown Rules:**
- Hard stop at -20% portfolio level
- Reduce position sizes during drawdowns
- Move to cash during regime changes

### 3. Regime Detection

Four Economic Regimes:
1. **Growth:** Stocks, corporate bonds, commodities
2. **Recession:** Treasuries, gold, defensive stocks
3. **Inflation:** Commodities, TIPS, real estate
4. **Deflation:** Long-term bonds, cash, quality stocks

### 4. Costs and Implementation

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Expense Ratio | Every 1% reduces returns by ~20% over 20 years |
| Turnover | Tax drag can reduce returns by 1-2% annually |
| Slippage | Can reduce returns by 0.5-2% for active strategies |

**Solution:**
- Use low-cost ETFs (expense ratio < 0.2%)
- Rebalance monthly (not daily)
- Use limit orders
- Trade liquid instruments
