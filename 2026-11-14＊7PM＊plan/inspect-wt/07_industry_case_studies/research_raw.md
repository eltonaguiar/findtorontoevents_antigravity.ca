## PART A — Famous / proven / contest-winning trading algorithms

### A1. TradingView ecosystem: famous indicators + what "contest" actually means

- **LazyBear "Squeeze Momentum Indicator" (2014)** — the single most famous TradingView community script; LazyBear states it is *"a derivative of John Carter's TTM Squeeze … as discussed in his book Mastering the Trade (chapter 11)"*. [TradingView script](https://www.tradingview.com/script/nqQ1DT5a-Squeeze-Momentum-Indicator-LazyBear/). A third-party catalog claimed 118,132 boosts (2026-11-11), but that number is from a low-authority site — `[UNVERIFIED]` ([source](https://proea.app/en/blog/squeeze-momentum-indicator-settings)).
- **everget "SuperTrend" (Mar 2019)** — ATR trailing stop, open source, GPL-3.0; GitHub repo ~965 stars. [TradingView script](https://www.tradingview.com/script/VLWVV7tH-SuperTrend-everget/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/everget/tradingview-pinescript-indicators). SuperTrend itself is credited by TradingView to **Olivier Seban** (~2009), not to everget ([TradingView help](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000634738-supertrend/), [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/supertrend-indicator-7976167)).
- **Other famous names**: KivancOzbilgic (v2 of LazyBear's squeeze), alexgrover (Pine Wizard), ChrisMoody, EXCAVO — popularity only, no performance record ([2025 Community Awards](https://www.tradingview.com/chart/SPX/g2U4uwkM-The-2025-TradingView-Community-Awards-Are-Official/)).
- **TradingView contests DO exist, but they are programming contests, not trading contests.** "Pinefest" launched 2023-09-19: winner gets **$500 + merch** for writing Pine code, judged on code/description quality — not on returns ([announcement](https://www.tradingview.com/blog/en/tradingview-pinefests-2023-40832/)). **Pinefest #1 winner: alexgrover**, "Intersection Value Functions" ([results](https://www.tradingview.com/chart/SPY/DiVF1Bwc-Pinefest-1-Winners/) · [winning script](https://www.tradingview.com/script/gD1gDOyI-Intersection-Value-Functions/)). Searches surfaced no official Pinefest #2+ results page — `[UNVERIFIED]` whether later editions ran.
- **Explicit statement:** I found **no TradingView official trading-performance contest and no TradingView-published live track record** for indicators/strategies. TradingView's Strategy Tester is a *backtest*; third-party backtest leaderboards (e.g. [TradeSearcher](https://tradesearcher.ai/)) rank backtests, not live money. Treat "TradingView contest winner = proven trader" as **false**.

### A4. Real-money trading contests: winners, and what happened after

- **World Cup Championship of Futures Trading (Robbins, run since 1983/84)** — real money, 12 months, statement-based standings. [Official historical standings](https://www.worldcupchampionships.com/world-cup-trading-championship-historical-standings) · [archived pre-2009 list](https://web.archive.org/web/20100106090542/www.robbinstrading.com/worldcup/standings.asp): **Larry Williams 1987 = 11,376%** (still the record), Ralph Casazzone 1985 = 1,283%, **Michelle Williams 1997 = 1,000%**, Chuck Hughes 1999 = 315%, Kurt Sakaeda 2000 = 595% / 2004 = 929%, John Holsinger 2001 = 608%, Michael Cook 2007 = 250%, Kevin Davey 2006 = 107%, **Andrea Unger 2008 = 672%**. Recent: **2024 Brent Carlile 532.3%**, 2024 FX Serghey Magala 201%, **2025 Tirutrade AG 324.7% (futures) / Pau Perdices Bellet 600.9% (forex)** ([year-by-year](https://trading-tournaments.com/articles/wctc-champions-year-by-year)).
- **What happened after — decay/lottery effects documented by the winners themselves:** Unger: *"It does not mean I achieve 672% every year… I would be a liar"*; the competition requires **risking up to 20% per trade vs 1-2% in sound live trading**, and he was at one point **47-48% in drawdown** ([Better System Trader interview](https://bettersystemtrader.com/016-andrea-unger/)). Kevin Davey's 2006 win: *"one copper trade blew the account upwards — without that trade I would not have won"* ([part 3](https://bettersystemtrader.com/trading-triumphs-kevin-daveys-journey-to-success-part-3/)). Median champion needs ~11%/month compounded; the record needs ~48.5%/month ([CTE research sheet PDF](https://completetradersedge.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CTE-Research-Sheet-World-Cup-Trading-Championships-2026.pdf)).
- **Rob Hoffman's wins are a different format**: single-elimination, front-of-audience, verified live accounts (Infinity Futures 2011; Salon du Trading Paris €50k, judges verify each trade) — see A2 links.
- **U.S. Investing Championship** (real-money, statement-verified): **Mark Minervini 1997 stocks = +155% on $250k** ([results](https://trading-tournaments.com/tournaments/usic-1997-stocks)), again **2021 = +334.8% ($1M+ division)** ([about](https://minerviniprivateaccess.com/about)). The organizer's own history: **1996 SEC investigation and a 1998 cease-and-desist against the contest operator's fund business**, then ~2 decades with no contest, restarted 2018 ([timeline](https://completetradersedge.com/us-investing-championship/)) — the *contest itself* needed a credibility repair.
- **"Wizard Cup": NOT FOUND** as a trading contest — searches returned an Australian-rules-football cup, a claw-machine arcade cup, and a sales competition. Do not cite it.
- **Kalshi**: no official contest; a **public real-money leaderboard** (profit $ / volume / predictions) exists ([kalshi.com/social/leaderboard](https://kalshi.com/social/leaderboard)). **Manifold**: leaderboards are **play-money (mana)**, plus sweepstakes prizes and a bot competition paying M$100k "mana" ([sweepstakes](https://news.manifold.markets/p/ramping-up-sweepstakes), [bot competition](https://manifold.markets/JamesGrugett/which-bots-will-win-the-manifold-tr)) — **not real money**.

### Synthesis — the 5 recurring patterns that separate "trusted with real money" from "backtest theater"

1. **A regulator or charter is the on-ramp, not a footnote.** Kalshi (CFTC DCM + court wins), Kraken (Wyoming SPDI), IEX (SEC national-exchange order), Alpaca (FINRA/SEC/SIPC) — the right to hold or route customer money is granted by someone with enforcement power, *before* scale.
2. **The core claim is verified by a third party, not by the firm.** Armanino's Merkle-tree PoR; WCTC's statement-verified live accounts; SAC's court-appointed compliance monitor; UBS/S&P paying a price an audit had to support. Self-published backtests are exactly what nobody accepts.
3. **Surviving a public drawdown, transparently, beats never having one.** Citadel's gate → high-water mark (checkable), AQR's value winter → record 2022 while investors still withdrew, Point72's ban → 2018 reopening. Silent decay is the signature of backtest theater.
4. **Model governance is the real moat — the inverse case proves it.** Two Sigma's four-year-unfixed model flaw cost $165M repayment + $90M penalty. Trusted shops have change control, kill switches, and audit trails *before* the regulator arrives; theater has only a Sharpe ratio.
5. **Time + distribution + audited reporting convert a track record into trust.** AQR needed ~25 years and a factor thesis it refused to abandon; Kensho and Wealthfront needed an institutional owner; Coinbase needed SEC-audited filings. Single-year contest wins (672%, 11,376%) are marketing, not evidence — the winners themselves say so.
