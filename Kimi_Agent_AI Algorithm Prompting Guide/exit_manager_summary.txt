KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ATR-BASED EXITS ARE SUPERIOR TO FIXED PERCENTAGE
   Research (Kaminski & Lo 2008, Snorrason & Yusupov 2009) shows:
   - Dynamic stops adapt to market volatility
   - Reduce premature exits during normal volatility
   - Provide better protection during high volatility
   - Your CL=F trade (+5.63%) using half-ATR trailing stop confirms this

2. PARTIAL PROFIT-TAKING IMPROVES RISK-ADJUSTED RETURNS
   - Locking in gains at 1x ATR reduces drawdowns
   - Allows remaining position to capture larger moves
   - Reduces emotional pressure on traders
   - Recommended: Close 50% at 1x ATR, trail remainder

3. VOLATILITY REGIME DETECTION IS CRITICAL
   - Markets cycle through compression, normal, expansion, high volatility
   - Stop distances should adjust: 0.85x in compression, 1.3x in high vol
   - VIX > 30 requires 30% wider stops and 40% position reduction

4. TRAILING STOP MECHANICS
   - Half-ATR (0.5x) trailing stop works well for most assets
   - Ratchet mechanism: only move stop in favorable direction
   - Gain-locking: Lock 50% of gains once achieved
   - Prevents giving back profits on reversals

IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CLASSES IMPLEMENTED:

1. ExitManager (Main Class)
   - initialize_position(): Creates position with calculated exit levels
   - process_bar(): Processes each price bar, checks all exit conditions
   - check_initial_stop(): ATR-based stop loss
   - check_take_profit(): ATR-based take profit
   - update_trailing_stop(): Half-ATR trailing with ratchet
   - check_partial_profit(): Multiple partial exit levels
   - check_time_exit(): Max hold period enforcement
   - check_breakeven(): Move stop to breakeven

2. VolatilityRegimeDetector
   - Detects 5 regimes: Compression, Normal, Expansion, High Vol, Exhaustion
   - Calculates ATR ratio vs baseline
   - Provides regime multiplier for stop adjustment
   - VIX integration for equity markets

3. ExitBacktester
   - run_backtest(): Simulates trades through completion
   - optimize_parameters(): Grid search for optimal parameters
   - Supports walk-forward analysis
