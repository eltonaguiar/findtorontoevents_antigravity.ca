5. What this page does NOT claim

  • No raw-alpha claim: the rotation does not beat SPY full-cycle in this window; the pilot is selection-based and sub-T2 (peers reproduced it; peers also correctly labeled it NOT PROVED).
  • Backtest is one path (2013–2026, one universe); momentum has documented but regime-dependent premia — no guarantee the next decade resembles this one.
  • Variant-C P/L came from the system's own ledger: slippage on closed rows is UNVERIFIED (QA Gate 1) and explicit intrabar-vs-nominal fill recording (B5) is still open.
  • Probability estimates are judgments, not frequencies — listed so they can be falsified.
  • Mutual funds remain structurally non-intraday (NAV clock): this sleeve is a monthly allocation engine, never a live TP/SL pick class (peer consensus, which I adopt).
