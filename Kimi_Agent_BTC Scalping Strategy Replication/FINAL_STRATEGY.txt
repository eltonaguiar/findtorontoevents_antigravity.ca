CORE CONCEPT: VWAP MEAN REVERSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY VWAP WORKS:
- VWAP = Volume Weighted Average Price
- Institutional traders use VWAP as fair value reference
- Price tends to revert to VWAP in range-bound markets
- In trends, VWAP acts as dynamic support/resistance

THE EDGE:
- When price extends >0.3% from VWAP, 70-80% chance of reversion
- When price is near VWAP, trend continuation is more likely
- Combining with regime detection improves accuracy

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ENTRY RULES (SIMPLIFIED - 4 CONDITIONS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LONG ENTRY (Buy):
  1. Price is BELOW VWAP by 0.15% to 0.40% (oversold)
  2. ADX < 25 (range market) OR price > VWAP in trend up
  3. Volume > 10 BTC/min (avoid illiquid periods)
  4. Not within 10 minutes of funding time

SHORT ENTRY (Sell):
  1. Price is ABOVE VWAP by 0.15% to 0.40% (overbought)
  2. ADX < 25 (range market) OR price < VWAP in trend down
  3. Volume > 10 BTC/min (avoid illiquid periods)
  4. Not within 10 minutes of funding time

WHY THESE RULES:
- VWAP distance: Sweet spot for mean reversion
- ADX filter: Avoid fighting strong trends
- Volume filter: Ensure liquidity for exits
- Funding filter: Avoid funding fee costs

EXIT RULES (CRITICAL FOR PROFITABILITY)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROFIT TARGETS:
  TP1: +0.20% → Close 50% of position → Move stop to breakeven
  TP2: +0.40% → Close 30% of position → Trailing stop activated
  TP3: +0.80% → Close remaining 20% (runner)

STOP LOSS:
  Initial: 0.15% from entry (tight but not too tight)
  Breakeven: Entry + 0.03% after TP1 hit
  Trailing: 0.15% below highest profit after TP2

TIME-BASED EXIT:
  Maximum hold time: 20 minutes
  If not profitable after 20 min, exit at market

WHY THESE EXITS:
- Scaled profit-taking captures gains at multiple levels
- Breakeven after TP1 creates "free trades"
- Time limit prevents hope-based holding
- Tight stops limit losses while allowing room for noise

POSITION SIZING (RISK-BASED)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BASE FORMULA:
  Position Size = (Account Risk % × Account Balance) / (Stop Loss % × Price)

EXAMPLE (10x leverage):
  Account: $10,000
  Risk per trade: 1% = $100
  Stop loss: 0.15%
  BTC Price: $70,000
  
  Position Size = ($100) / (0.0015 × $70,000) = $100 / $105 = 0.95 BTC

RISK RULES:

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MARKET REGIME DETECTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HOW TO DETECT REGIME:

RANGE MARKET (ADX < 25):
  → Fade extremes (buy below VWAP, sell above VWAP)
  → Tighter profit targets (0.20%, 0.40%)
  → More selective entries

TRENDING MARKET (ADX > 25):
  → Follow the trend
  → Uptrend: Buy pullbacks to VWAP
  → Downtrend: Sell rallies to VWAP
  → Wider profit targets (0.30%, 0.60%)

WHY THIS MATTERS:
- Range strategy in trending market = losses
- Trend strategy in ranging market = whipsaws
- Regime detection improves win rate by 15-20%
