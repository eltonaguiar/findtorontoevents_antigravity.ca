## 3. NEW findings and corrections to the known list

2. **`timeout-minutes: 115` is undersized** (`audit-dashboard.yml:55`): nearest-rank p95 is 119.1 and one run was marked `cancelled` after all 54 steps reported success. (`GA4`)
